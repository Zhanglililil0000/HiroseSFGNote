# B022：附录B分批校订

## 附录 B：含时相互作用微扰论中的展开系数

> **译者注（Q022）：** 式（B-1）、（B-2）按底本保留 $r_{nm}$ 及系数下标，部分指标与左端是否相容仍需推导核验；不据对称性静默替换。式（B-8）末项第二个指数已对照底本改为 $e^{+i(\omega_1+\omega_2)t}$，旧译误写负号。

将（A-1）式两边分别左乘 $\Psi_n^*$ 和 $\Psi_l^*$，并对全空间积分，可得：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\dot{c}(t)_{n,n} = -\frac{i}{\hbar} \sum_{m \neq n} c(t)_{m,n} r_{nm} \bigl( e^{i[\omega - (\omega_m - \omega_n)]t} + e^{-i[\omega + (\omega_m - \omega_n)]t} \bigr)\\[6pt]
&\dot{c}(t)_{l,n} = -\frac{i}{\hbar} \bigl\{ c(t)_{n,n} r_{nm} \bigl( e^{i[\omega - (\omega_n - \omega_l)]t} + e^{-i[\omega + (\omega_n - \omega_l)]t} \bigr) + \sum_{m \neq n} c(t)_{m,l} r_{lm} \bigl( e^{i[\omega - (\omega_m - \omega_l)]t}\\[6pt]
&+ e^{-i[\omega + (\omega_m - \omega_l)]t} \bigr) \bigr\}\\[6pt]
&(n \neq m, m \neq l, n \neq l)
\end{aligned}
\tag{B-1}
$$

假设 $r_{nm}$ 为一阶小量，对 $c(t)_{n,n}$ 和 $c(t)_{m,n}$ 进行微扰展开，可得：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\dot{c}(t)_{n,n} = -\frac{i}{\hbar} \bigl[ c_{n,n}^{(1)}(t) + c_{n,n}^{(2)}(t) + c_{n,n}^{(3)}(t) + \cdots \bigr] \sum_{m \neq n} r_{nm} \bigl( e^{i[\omega - (\omega_m - \omega_n)]t} + e^{-i[\omega + (\omega_m - \omega_n)]t} \bigr)\\[6pt]
&\dot{c}(t)_{l,n} = -\frac{i}{\hbar} \Bigg\{ \bigl[ 1 + c_{n,n}^{(1)}(t) + c_{n,n}^{(2)}(t) + c_{n,n}^{(3)}(t) + \cdots \bigr] r_{nm} \bigl( e^{i[\omega - (\omega_n - \omega_l)]t} + e^{-i[\omega + (\omega_n - \omega_l)]t} \bigr)\\[6pt]
&\quad + \sum_{m \neq n} \bigl[ c_{m,n}^{(1)}(t) + c_{m,n}^{(2)}(t) + c_{m,n}^{(3)}(t) + \cdots \bigr] r_{lm} \bigl( e^{i[\omega - (\omega_m - \omega_l)]t} + e^{-i[\omega + (\omega_m - \omega_l)]t} \bigr) \Bigg\}
\end{aligned}
\tag{B-2}
$$

通过逐次近似求解，可得以下结果：

**（一阶近似）**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&c(t)_{l,n}^{(1)} = \frac{r_{ln}}{\hbar} \bigl[ \frac{e^{-i(\omega - \omega_{ln})t}}{\omega - \omega_{ln}} - \frac{e^{i(\omega + \omega_{ln})t}}{\omega + \omega_{ln}} \bigr], \quad (l \neq n)\\[6pt]
&c(t)_{n,n}^{(1)} = 0
\end{aligned}
\tag{B-3}
$$

**（二阶近似）**

对时间微分得到以下方程：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\dot{c}(t)_{n,n} = -\frac{i}{\hbar} \sum_{m \neq n} c_{m,n}^{(1)}(t) r_{nm} \bigl( e^{i(\omega - \omega_{mn})t} + e^{-i(\omega + \omega_{mn})t} \bigr)\\[6pt]
&= \frac{i}{\hbar^2} \sum_{m \neq n} r_{nm} r_{mn} \bigl[ \frac{1 + e^{2i\omega t}}{\omega + \omega_{mn}} - \frac{1 + e^{-2i\omega t}}{\omega - \omega_{mn}} \bigr], \quad (\omega \neq \omega_{nm})
\end{aligned}
\tag{B-4}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\dot{c}(t)_{l,n} = \frac{i}{\hbar^2} \sum_{m \neq n} r_{lm} r_{mn} e^{i\omega_{ln}t} \bigl[ \frac{1 + e^{2i\omega t}}{\omega + \omega_{mn}} - \frac{1 + e^{-2i\omega t}}{\omega - \omega_{mn}} \bigr], \quad (l \neq n, \omega \neq \omega_{mn}, \omega \neq \omega_{lm})
\end{aligned}
\tag{B-5}
$$

由此得：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&c_{n,n}^{(2)}(t) = -\frac{1}{\hbar^2} \sum_{m \neq n} r_{nm} r_{mn} \bigl[ \frac{1}{\omega - \omega_{mn}} \bigl( it - \frac{e^{-2i\omega t}}{2\omega} \bigr) - \frac{1}{\omega + \omega_{mn}} \bigl( it + \frac{e^{2i\omega t}}{2\omega} \bigr) \bigr], \quad (\omega \neq \omega_{nm})
\end{aligned}
\tag{B-6}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&c_{l,n}^{(2)}(t) = \frac{1}{\hbar^2} \sum_{m \neq n} r_{lm} r_{mn} e^{i\omega_{ln}t} \bigl[ \frac{1}{\omega + \omega_{mn}} \bigl( \frac{1}{\omega_{ln}} + \frac{e^{2i\omega t}}{2\omega + \omega_{ln}} \bigr) - \frac{1}{\omega - \omega_{mn}} \bigl( \frac{1}{\omega_{ln}}\\[6pt]
&- \frac{e^{-2i\omega t}}{2\omega - \omega_{ln}} \bigr) \bigr]\\[6pt]
&\quad (l \neq n, \omega \neq \omega_{mn}, \omega \neq \omega_{lm})
\end{aligned}
\tag{B-7}
$$
