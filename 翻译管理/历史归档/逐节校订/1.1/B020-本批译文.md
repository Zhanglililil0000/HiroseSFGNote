$$
\begin{aligned}

&\quad\quad\quad + \frac{1}{\omega_2 + \omega_{mn}} \bigl( \frac{1}{\omega_{ln}} + \frac{e^{2i\omega_2 t}}{2\omega_2 + \omega_{ln}} \bigr) - \frac{1}{\omega_2 - \omega_{mn}} \bigl( \frac{1}{\omega_{ln}} - \frac{e^{-2i\omega_2 t}}{2\omega_2 - \omega_{ln}} \bigr) \Bigg]\\[6pt]
&\quad\quad\quad + e^{i\omega_{ml} t} \Bigg[ \frac{e^{i(\omega_1 - \omega_2)t}}{\omega_1 - \omega_2 + \omega_{ml}} \bigl( -\frac{1}{\omega_2 - \omega_{mn}} + \frac{1}{\omega_1 + \omega_{mn}} \bigr) + \frac{e^{-i(\omega_1 - \omega_2)t}}{\omega_1 - \omega_2 - \omega_{ml}} \bigl(\\[6pt]

由上述结果可见，在一阶近似下，当分子能级间隔与光子能量一致且能级之间的坐标矩阵元不为零时，跃迁概率会显著增加。此外，即使能量不完全匹配，仍存在一定的值，这与拉曼散射有关。在二阶近似下，当光子能量的两倍、和或差与跃迁能量一致时，跃迁概率同样会显著增加。

入射光电场在分子中引起的极化 $p(t)$ 由 $\langle p(t) \rangle = e \langle \Psi(r,t) | r | \Psi(r,t) \rangle$ 给出。即：

**（一阶近似）**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&p(t)^{(1)} = \sum_{m \neq n} \bigl( c(t)_{m,n}^{(1)} r_{nm} + c(t)_{m,n}^{(1)*} r_{mn} \bigr)\\[6pt]
&= \frac{1}{\hbar} \sum_{m \neq n} r_{nm} r_{mn} \bigl[ \frac{e^{i(\omega - \omega_{ln})t} + e^{-i(\omega - \omega_{ln})t}}{\omega - \omega_{ln}} - \frac{e^{i(\omega + \omega_{ln})t} + e^{-i(\omega + \omega_{ln})t}}{\omega + \omega_{ln}} \bigr]
\end{aligned}
\tag{A-9}
$$
