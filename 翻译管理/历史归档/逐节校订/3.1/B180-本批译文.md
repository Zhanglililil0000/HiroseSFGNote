**m'/m 界面的 m 侧：**

（a）：$E^+$ 源与 $E^-$ 源（经反射）。

$$
\begin{aligned}
&P_a^{*}(h_{m}^{+}) \\ &=\sum_{\alpha,\beta} \chi_{a\alpha\beta} E^{0}_{vis,\alpha}E^{0}_{ir,\beta}\\[4pt]&\quad \times \frac{t_{1m',vis,\alpha}t_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}(1 + r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}})}{{\begin{aligned}&\begin{aligned}&1\\&+r_{1m',vis,\alpha}r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}\\&\quad\\&+(r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}\end{aligned}\end{aligned}}}\\[4pt]&\quad \times \frac{t_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}(1+ r_{m'm,ir,\beta})(1+ r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}})}{{\begin{aligned}&\begin{aligned}&1\\&+r_{1m',ir,\beta}r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}\\&\quad\\&+(r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}\end{aligned}\end{aligned}}}
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.25}
$$

（b）：$E^+$ 源（经反射）与 $E^-$ 源。

$$
\begin{aligned}
&P_a^{*}(h_{m}^{+}) \\ &=\sum_{\alpha,\beta} \chi_{a\alpha\beta} E^{0}_{vis,\alpha}E^{0}_{ir,\beta}\\[4pt]&\quad \times \frac{t_{1m',vis,\alpha}t_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}(1 + r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}})}{{\begin{aligned}&\begin{aligned}&1\\&+r_{1m',vis,\alpha}r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}\\&\quad\\&+(r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}\end{aligned}\end{aligned}}}\\[4pt]&\quad \times \frac{t_{1m',ir,\beta}t_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}(1+ r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}})}{{\begin{aligned}&\begin{aligned}&1\\&+r_{1m',ir,\beta}r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}\\&\quad\\&+(r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}\end{aligned}\end{aligned}}}
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.26}
$$



> **原文疑点（Q103，PDF p11，式2.25）：** 红外光分子仍采用 $(1+r_{m\prime m,ir,\beta})$，与同式可见光分子及2.26的对应透射系数不同，沿原式保留。候选为改用对应透射系数，关联Q098，未采用。
