### 2.3 和频电场

利用另一文件「双层膜的和频(和频)——一般式」中的结果，可以导出以下表达式。此外，关于相位部分仅记其结果。

**1/m' 界面 1 侧极化所产生的 和频：**

(a): 反射方向

零次光产生的 $E^{-}(0^{-}) + r_{1m'}E^{+}(0^{-})$ 之外，还需加上进入内部的光经多重反射后出射的分量。也就是说，将文件「双层膜的和频(和频)——一般式」（以下简称文件「一般式」）的 (2.1) 式中的 $L^{+}_{m'/m'}P(z_1)$ 置换为 $t_{1m'}L^{+}_{1/1}P(z_1 = 0)$ 后所得的分量。由 $a_0^{*} = a$、$a_0 = 1$，得到下式：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E^{-}_{1}(0^{-})_{\mathrm{net}} \\ &= \{L^{-}_{1/1} + L^{+}_{1/1}[r_{1m'} + \frac{t_{1m'}t_{m'1}(r_{m'm} + r_{m2}b^{2})a^{2}}{1+ r_{1m'}r_{m'm}a^{2} + (r_{m'm} + r_{1m'}a^{2})r_{m2}b^{2}}]\}P^{*}(z_1 = 0)
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \frac{(L^{-}_{1/1} + r_{1m'}L^{+}_{1/1})(1 + r_{m'm}r_{m2}b^{2}) + (r_{1m'}L^{-}_{1/1} + L^{+}_{1/1})(r_{m'm} + r_{m2}b^{2})a^{2}}{1 + r_{1m'}r_{m'm}a^{2} + (r_{m'm} + r_{1m'}a^{2})r_{m2}b^{2}} P^{*}(z_1 = 0)
\end{aligned}
$$

其中，

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(L^{-}_{1/1,px} + r_{1m',p}L^{+}_{1/1,px}) \\ &= (r_{1m',p}L^{-}_{1/1,px} + L^{+}_{1/1,px}) = L^{-}_{1/m',px}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(L^{-}_{1/1,sy} + r_{1m',s}L^{+}_{1/1,sy}) \\ &= (r_{1m',s}L^{-}_{1/1,sy} + L^{+}_{1/1,sy}) = L^{-}_{1/m',sy}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(L^{-}_{1/1,pz} + r_{1m',p}L^{+}_{1/1,pz}) \\ &= -(r_{1m',p}L^{-}_{1/1,pz} + L^{+}_{1/1,pz}) = L^{-}_{1/m',pz} \quad (n_{m"} = n_1)
\end{aligned}
$$

因此，

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E^{-}_{1}(0^{-})_{\mathrm{net}} \\ &= \frac{(1+ r_{m'm}r_{m2}b^{2}) \pm (r_{m'm} + r_{m2}b^{2})a^{2}}{1 + r_{1m'}r_{m'm}a^{2} + (r_{m'm} + r_{1m'}a^{2})r_{m2}b^{2}} L^{-}_{1/m'}P^{*}(z_1 = 0)
\end{aligned}
$$

(式中上符号对应 x 和 y 分量，下符号对应 z 分量)

代入 (2.18) 式，得：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E^{-}_{1}(0^{-})_{\mathrm{net},p} \\ &= \sum_{\alpha,\beta} E^{0}_{vis,\alpha}E^{0}_{ir,\beta}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1+ r_{1m',vis,\alpha})[(1 + r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}) + (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}]}{{\begin{aligned}&1\\&+ r_{1m',vis,\alpha}r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}\\&+ (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}\end{aligned}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta})[(1+ r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}) + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}]}{1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta}r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times[

\frac{(1 + r_{m'm,SF,p}r_{m2,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}}) + (r_{m'm,SF,p} + r_{m2,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}})e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}}}{1+ r_{1m',SF,p}r_{m'm,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,SF,p} + r_{1m',SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}})r_{m2,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}}} L^{-}_{1/m',px}\chi_{x\alpha\beta}


\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&+

\frac{(1 + r_{m'm,SF,p}r_{m2,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}}) - (r_{m'm,SF,p} + r_{m2,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}})e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}}}{1 + r_{1m',SF,p}r_{m'm,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,SF,p} + r_{1m',SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}})r_{m2,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}}} L^{-}_{1/m',pz}\chi_{z\alpha\beta}] \quad (n_{m"} \\ &= n_1) 
\end{aligned}\tag{2.31a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E^{-}_{1}(0^{-})_{\mathrm{net},s} \\ &= \sum_{\alpha,\beta} E^{0}_{vis,\alpha}E^{0}_{ir,\beta}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1+ r_{1m',vis,\alpha})[(1 + r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}) + (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}]}{{\begin{aligned}&1\\&+ r_{1m',vis,\alpha}r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}\\&+ (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}\end{aligned}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta})[(1+ r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}) + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}]}{1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta}r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1 + r_{m'm,SF,s}r_{m2,SF,s}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}}) + (r_{m'm,SF,s} + r_{m2,SF,s}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}})e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}}}{1+ r_{1m',SF,s}r_{m'm,SF,s}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,SF,s} + r_{1m',SF,s}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}})r_{m2,SF,s}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}}} L^{-}_{1/m',sy}\chi_{y\alpha\beta} 
\end{aligned}\tag{2.31b}
$$

(b): 透射方向

仅有 $E^{+}(0^{-})$ 进入膜内并穿透至对面。与上文同理，将文件「一般式」的 (2.2) 式中的 $L^{+}_{m'/m'}P(z_1)$ 置换为 $t_{1m'}L^{+}_{1/1}P(z_1 = 0)$，并令 $a_0^{*} = a$、$a_0 = 1$，得到下式：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E^{+}_{2}(h_{m}^{+})_{\mathrm{net}} \\ &= \frac{t_{1m'}t_{m2}t_{m'm}ab}{1+ r_{1m'}r_{m'm}a^{2} + (r_{m'm} + r_{1m'}a^{2})r_{m2}b^{2}} L^{+}_{1/1}P^{*}(z_1 = 0)
\end{aligned}
$$

其中，

$$
\begin{aligned}
&t_{1m',p}L^{+}_{1/1,px} \\ &= L^{+}_{1/m',px}, \quad
t_{1m',s}L^{+}_{1/1,sy} = L^{+}_{1/m',sy}, \quad
t_{1m',p}L^{+}_{1/1,pz} = L^{+}_{1/m',pz} \quad (n_{m"} = n_1)
\end{aligned}
$$

因此，

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E^{+}_{2}(h_{m}^{+})_{\mathrm{net}} \\ &= \frac{t_{m'm}t_{m2}ab}{1+ r_{1m'}r_{m'm}a^{2} + (r_{m'm} + r_{1m'}a^{2})r_{m2}b^{2}} L^{+}_{1/m'}P^{*}(z_1 = 0)
\end{aligned}
$$

代入 (2.18) 式，得：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E^{+}_{2}(h_{m}^{+})_{\mathrm{net},p} \\ &= \sum_{\alpha,\beta} E^{0}_{vis,\alpha}E^{0}_{ir,\beta}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1+ r_{1m',vis,\alpha})[(1 + r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}) + (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}]}{{\begin{aligned}&1\\&+ r_{1m',vis,\alpha}r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}\\&+ (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}\end{aligned}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta})[(1+ r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}) + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}]}{1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta}r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{t_{1m',SF,p}t_{m2,SF,p}e^{i(\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}+\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'})}(L^{+}_{1/m',px}\chi_{x\alpha\beta} + L^{+}_{1/m',pz}\chi_{z\alpha\beta})}{1+ r_{1m',SF,p}r_{m'm,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,SF,p} + r_{1m',SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}})r_{m2,SF,p}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}}} \quad (n_{m"}=n_1) 
\end{aligned}\tag{2.32a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E^{+}_{2}(h_{m}^{+})_{\mathrm{net},s} \\ &= \sum_{\alpha,\beta} E^{0}_{vis,\alpha}E^{0}_{ir,\beta}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1+ r_{1m',vis,\alpha})[(1 + r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}) + (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}]}{{\begin{aligned}&1\\&+ r_{1m',vis,\alpha}r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}\\&+ (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}\end{aligned}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{(1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta})[(1+ r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}) + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}]}{1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta}r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\\[4pt]&\times

\frac{t_{1m',SF,s}t_{m2,SF,s}e^{i(\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}+\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'})}L^{+}_{1/m',sy}\chi_{y\alpha\beta}}{1+ r_{1m',SF,s}r_{m'm,SF,s}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}} + (r_{m'm,SF,s} + r_{1m',SF,s}e^{2i\beta_{m',SF}h_{m'}})r_{m2,SF,s}e^{2i\beta_{m,SF}h_{m}}} 
\end{aligned}\tag{2.32b}
