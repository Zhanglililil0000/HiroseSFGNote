**m/2界面的2侧：**

（a）：$E^+$ 源。

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_a^{*}(h_{m'} + h_{m}^{+}) &=\sum_{\alpha,\beta} \chi_{a\alpha\beta} E^{0}_{vis,\alpha}E^{0}_{ir,\beta}\\[6pt]&\times \frac{t_{1m',vis,\alpha}t_{m'm,vis,\alpha}t_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{i(\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}+\beta_{m,vis}h_{m})}}{\begin{aligned}&1+r_{1m',vis,\alpha}r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}\\&\quad+(r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}\end{aligned}}\\[6pt]&\times \frac{t_{1m',ir,\beta}t_{m'm,ir,\beta}t_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{i(\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}+\beta_{m,ir}h_{m})}}{\begin{aligned}&1+r_{1m',ir,\beta}r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}\\&\quad+(r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}\end{aligned}}
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.20}
$$

（b）：$E^-$ 源（$n>0$ 时，经反射）。

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_a^{*}(h_{m'} + h_{m}^{+}) &=\sum_{\alpha,\beta} \chi_{a\alpha\beta} E^{0}_{vis,\alpha}E^{0}_{ir,\beta}\\[6pt]&\times \frac{t_{1m',vis,\alpha}t_{m'm,vis,\alpha}t_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{i(\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}+\beta_{m,vis}h_{m})}}{\begin{aligned}&1+r_{1m',vis,\alpha}r_{m'm,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}}\\&\quad+(r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m',vis}h_{m'}})r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_{m}}\end{aligned}}\\[6pt]&\times \frac{t_{1m',ir,\beta}t_{m'm,ir,\beta}t_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{i(\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}+\beta_{m,ir}h_{m})}}{\begin{aligned}&1+r_{1m',ir,\beta}r_{m'm,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}}\\&\quad+(r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m',ir}h_{m'}})r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_{m}}\end{aligned}}
