### 4.1. 单层薄膜（1/m/2）中的薄膜内电场

由式 (2.7b) 和式 (3.2)，用下标 $m$ 表示薄膜，则

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-(0) &= -\frac{r_{m1} + r_{2m} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}}{1 + r_{m1} r_{2m} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}} E^+(0) \\
&= \frac{r_{1m} + r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}} E^+(0) \tag{4.3a}
\end{aligned}
$$

薄膜内部从入射点进入 $z_1$ 处的电场为：

$$

\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E^+(z_1)\\[6pt]
E^-(z_1)\end{bmatrix}
&= \frac{1}{t_{10}} \begin{bmatrix}e^{i\beta_m z_1} & r_{10} e^{i\beta_m z_1}\\[6pt]
r_{10} e^{-i\beta_m z_1} & e^{-i\beta_m z_1}\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}E^+(0)\\[6pt]
E^-(0)\end{bmatrix} \\[8pt]
&= \frac{1}{t_{m1}(1 + r_{m1} r_{2m} e^{2i\beta_m h_m})} \begin{bmatrix}(1 - r_{m1}^2) e^{i\beta_m z_1}\\[6pt]
-r_{2m}(1 - r_{m1}^2) e^{2i\beta_1 h_1} e^{-i\beta_1 z_1}\end{bmatrix} E^+(0) \\[8pt]
&= \frac{t_{1m}}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}} \begin{bmatrix}e^{i\beta_m z_1}\\[6pt]
r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_1 h_1} e^{-i\beta_1 z_1}\end{bmatrix} E^+(0) \tag{4.4a}
\end{aligned}

$$

从出射点进入介质 2 中 $z_2$ 处的电场为：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+(z_2) &= \frac{(1 - r_{m1}^2)(1 - r_{2m}^2) e^{i\beta_m h_m}}{t_{m1} t_{2m}(1 + r_{m1} r_{2m} e^{2i\beta_m h_m})} e^{i\beta_2 z_2} E^+(0)
\\
&= \frac{t_{1m} t_{m2} e^{i\beta_m h_m}}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}} e^{i\beta_2 z_2} E^+(0) \tag{4.5a}
\end{aligned}
$$

式 (4.3a) 和式 (4.4a) 与考虑了多重反射时的反射光及透射光振幅表达式一致。由式 (4.3a) ~ (4.5a) 可得：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E^+(0) + E^-(0)\\[6pt]
E^+(0) - E^-(0)\end{bmatrix}
&= \frac{1}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}} \begin{bmatrix}(1 + r_{1m})(1 + r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m})\\[6pt]
(1 - r_{1m})(1 - r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m})\end{bmatrix} E^+(0) \tag{4.3b}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E^+(z_1) + E^-(z_1)\\[6pt]
E^+(z_1) - E^-(z_1)\end{bmatrix}
&= \frac{t_{1m}}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}} \begin{bmatrix}e^{i\beta_m z_1} + r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_1 h_1} e^{-i\beta_1 z_1}\\[6pt]
e^{i\beta_m z_1} - r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_1 h_1} e^{-i\beta_1 z_1}\end{bmatrix} E^+(0) \tag{4.4b}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E^+(z_2) + E^-(z_2)\\[6pt]
E^+(z_2) - E^-(z_2)\end{bmatrix}
&= \frac{t_{1m} t_{m2} e^{i\beta_m h_m}}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2} e^{2i\beta_m h_m}} \begin{bmatrix}e^{i\beta_2 z_2}\\[6pt]
e^{i\beta_2 z_2}\end{bmatrix} E^+(0) \tag{4.5b}
\end{aligned}
$$

在薄膜厚度为零的极限（$h_m \to 0$）下，变为：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E^+(0) + E^-(0)\\[6pt]
E^+(0) - E^-(0)\end{bmatrix}
&= \frac{1}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2}} \begin{bmatrix}(1 + r_{1m})(1 + r_{m2})\\[6pt]
(1 - r_{1m})(1 - r_{m2})\end{bmatrix} E^+(0) \tag{4.6a}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E^+(z_1) + E^-(z_1)\\[6pt]
E^+(z_1) - E^-(z_1)\end{bmatrix}
&= \frac{t_{1m}}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2}} \begin{bmatrix}1 + r_{m2}\\[6pt]
1 - r_{m2}\end{bmatrix} E^+(0) \tag{4.6b}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E^+(z_2) + E^-(z_2)\\[6pt]
E^+(z_2) - E^-(z_2)\end{bmatrix}
&= \frac{t_{1m} t_{m2}}{1 + r_{1m} r_{m2}} \begin{bmatrix}e^{i\beta_2 z_2}\\[6pt]
e^{i\beta_2 z_2}\end{bmatrix} E^+(0) \tag{4.6c}
\end{aligned}
$$

和频极化由入射红外光和可见光的电场 $E^+$ 与 $E^-$ 之和产生。对于 p 偏振光，利用 $E_z^+ + E_z^- = -(E_x^+ - E_x^-)(\sin\theta/\cos\theta)$ 的关系来求 $z$ 分量之和。



> **原文记号疑点（Q091，PDF p7—8）**：4.4a/b在m层记法中仍混用 $r_{10},t_{10},\beta_1h_1,\beta_1z_1$；正文又把4.4a称为透射光振幅式，尽管其列出的是膜内场。这里均按原文保留；将层号1等同m、或将该引用改为4.5a只是候选。零厚度极限4.6c及p偏振z分量说明跨至p8，已接全。
