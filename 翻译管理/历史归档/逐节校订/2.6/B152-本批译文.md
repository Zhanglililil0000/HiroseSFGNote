$$

\begin{bmatrix} U^{a} \\ V^{a} \end{bmatrix} = (\mathbf{L}^{a})^{-1} \begin{bmatrix} E_a^+ \\ E_a^- \end{bmatrix},\quad
\begin{bmatrix} E_a^+ \\ E_a^- \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{L}^{a} \begin{bmatrix} U^{a} \\ V^{a} \end{bmatrix}\quad (a = x, y)

$$

则式 (2.2a) 和式 (2.2b) 可改写为如下形式。

$$

\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E_a^+(0)\\[6pt]
E_a^-(0)\end{bmatrix}
&= \mathbf{L}_0^{a} \begin{bmatrix}U^{a}(0)\\[6pt]
V^{a}(0)\end{bmatrix}
= \mathbf{L}_0^{a} \mathbf{M}_1^{a} \begin{bmatrix}U^{a}(1)\\[6pt]
V^{a}(1)\end{bmatrix}
= \mathbf{L}_0^{a} \mathbf{M}_1^{a} (\mathbf{L}_1^{a})^{-1} \begin{bmatrix}E_a^+(1)\\[6pt]
E_a^-(1)\end{bmatrix} \\[8pt]
&= [\mathbf{L}_0^{a} \mathbf{M}_1^{a} (\mathbf{L}_1^{a})^{-1}][\mathbf{L}_1^{a} \mathbf{M}_2^{a} (\mathbf{L}_2^{a})^{-1}] \cdots [\mathbf{L}_{n-1}^{a} \mathbf{M}_n^{a} (\mathbf{L}_n^{a})^{-1}] \begin{bmatrix}E_a^+(n)\\[6pt]
E_a^-(n)\end{bmatrix} \\[8pt]
&= \boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}}_1^{a} \boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}}_2^{a} \cdots \boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}}_n^{a} \begin{bmatrix}E_a^+(n)\\[6pt]
E_a^-(n)\end{bmatrix} \tag{2.7a}
\end{aligned}

$$

$$

\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}E_a^+(n)\\[6pt]
E_a^-(n)\end{bmatrix}
&= \mathbf{L}_n^{a} \begin{bmatrix}U^{a}(n)\\[6pt]
V^{a}(n)\end{bmatrix}
= \mathbf{L}_n^{a} \mathbf{N}_n^{a} \begin{bmatrix}U^{a}(n-1)\\[6pt]
V^{a}(n-1)\end{bmatrix}
= \mathbf{L}_n^{a} \mathbf{N}_n^{a} (\mathbf{L}_{n-1}^{a})^{-1} \begin{bmatrix}E_a^+(n-1)\\[6pt]
E_a^-(n-1)\end{bmatrix} \\[8pt]
&= [\mathbf{L}_n^{a} \mathbf{N}_n^{a} (\mathbf{L}_{n-1}^{a})^{-1}][\mathbf{L}_{n-1}^{a} \mathbf{N}_{n-1}^{a} (\mathbf{L}_{n-2}^{a})^{-1}] \cdots [\mathbf{L}_1^{a} \mathbf{N}_1^{a} (\mathbf{L}_0^{a})^{-1}] \begin{bmatrix}E_a^+(0)\\[6pt]
E_a^-(0)\end{bmatrix} \\[8pt]
&= \boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_n^{a} \boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_{n-1}^{a} \cdots \boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_1^{a} \boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_0^{a} \begin{bmatrix}E_a^+(0)\\[6pt]
E_a^-(0)\end{bmatrix} \tag{2.7b}
\end{aligned}

$$



> **原文疑点（Q089，PDF p4，式2.7b）**：最后一行在 $\mathcal N_1^a$ 后另有 $\mathcal N_0^a$，而前一行展开至 $L_1^aN_1^a(L_0^a)^{-1}$。此处保留多出的0层因子；删去它只是候选。为区分底本中转换前后两套不同字形的M/N，本译文用粗体M/N与花体 $\mathcal M,\mathcal N$，层号为下标、分组a为上标，不新增物理量。
