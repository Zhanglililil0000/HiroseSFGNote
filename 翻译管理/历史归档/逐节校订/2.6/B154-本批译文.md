## 3. 界面数量与传播矩阵

将介质从入射侧开始依次编号为 0, 1, 2, ...，若界面数在 2 个以上，则中间各层具有确定的厚度。设入射光经过 $n$ 种介质后出射，中途通过的各层薄膜厚度为 $h_1, h_2, \dots, h_{n-1}$。

### 3.1. 界面为 1 个的情况

$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_1^{y} &= \mathbf{L}_1^{y} \mathbf{N}_1^{y} (\mathbf{L}_0^{y})^{-1}
\\
&= \frac{1}{t_{10,y}} \begin{bmatrix}e^{i\beta_1 z_1} & r_{10,s} e^{i\beta_1 z_1}\\[6pt]
r_{10,s} e^{-i\beta_1 z_1} & e^{-i\beta_1 z_1}\end{bmatrix} \tag{3.1a}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_1^{x} &= \mathbf{L}_1^{x} \mathbf{N}_1^{x} (\mathbf{L}_0^{x})^{-1}
\\
&= \frac{1}{t_{10,x}} \begin{bmatrix}e^{i\beta_1 z_1} & r_{10,p} e^{i\beta_1 z_1}\\[6pt]
r_{10,p} e^{-i\beta_1 z_1} & e^{-i\beta_1 z_1}\end{bmatrix} \tag{3.1b}
\end{aligned}
$$

### 3.2. 界面为 2 个的情况

$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_2^{y} \boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_1^{y} &= \frac{1}{t_{21,y} t_{10,y}} \begin{bmatrix}(e^{i\beta_1 h_1} + r_{21,s} r_{10,s} e^{-i\beta_1 h_1}) e^{i\beta_2 z_2} & (r_{10,s} e^{i\beta_1 h_1} + r_{21,s} e^{-i\beta_1 h_1}) e^{i\beta_2 z_2}\\[6pt]
(r_{21,s} e^{i\beta_1 h_1} + r_{10,s} e^{-i\beta_1 h_1}) e^{-i\beta_2 z_2} & (r_{21,s} r_{10,s} e^{i\beta_1 h_1} + e^{-i\beta_1 h_1}) e^{-i\beta_2 z_2}\end{bmatrix} \tag{3.2a}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_2^{x} \boldsymbol{\mathcal{N}}_1^{x} &= \frac{1}{t_{21,x} t_{10,x}} \begin{bmatrix}(e^{i\beta_1 h_1} + r_{21,p} r_{10,p} e^{-i\beta_1 h_1}) e^{i\beta_2 z_2} & (r_{10,p} e^{i\beta_1 h_1} + r_{21,p} e^{-i\beta_1 h_1}) e^{i\beta_2 z_2}\\[6pt]
(r_{21,p} e^{i\beta_1 h_1} + r_{10,p} e^{-i\beta_1 h_1}) e^{-i\beta_2 z_2} & (r_{21,p} r_{10,p} e^{i\beta_1 h_1} + e^{-i\beta_1 h_1}) e^{-i\beta_2 z_2}\end{bmatrix} \tag{3.2b}
\end{aligned}
$$
