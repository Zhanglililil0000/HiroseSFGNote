# B030：1.2分批校订

### 三重简并
H. H. Nielsen 使用球坐标系，令 $ q_{t1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \cos \chi_t $、$q_{t2} = r_t \sin \theta_t \sin \chi_t$、$q_{t3} = r_t \cos \theta_t$，并进一步变形为 $ q_t^0 = r_t \cos \theta_t  $、$  q_t^{\pm 1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(\pm i \chi_t)$，由此得到以下矩阵元素。量子数包括径向量子数 $v_t$、对应于总角动量的 $l_t$、以及磁量子数 $m_t$（其中 $l_t = v_t, v_t - 2$,$ \dots$,  $1 \text{ 或 } 0$，$m_t = l_t$, $l_t - 1$, $l_t - 2$,$ \dots, -l_t$）。
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\langle v_t, l_t, m_t | q_t^0 = r_t \cos \theta_t | v_t - 1, l_t - 1, m_t \rangle = \langle v_t - 1, l_t - 1, m_t | q_t^0 | v_t, l_t, m_t \rangle\\[6pt]
&= \sqrt{\frac{(v_t + l_t + 2)}{2}} \sqrt{\frac{(l_t - m_t)(l_t + m_t)}{(2l_t - 1)(2l_t + 1)}}, \quad (\Delta v_t = \pm 1, \Delta l_t = \pm 1, \Delta m_t = 0)
\end{aligned}

$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\langle v_t, l_t, m_t | q_t^0 | v_t - 1, l_t + 1, m_t \rangle = \langle v_t - 1, l_t + 1, m_t | q_t^0 | v_t, l_t, m_t \rangle\\[6pt]
&= + \sqrt{\frac{(v_t - l_t)}{2}} \sqrt{\frac{(l_t - m_t + 1)(l_t + m_t + 1)}{(2l_t + 1)(2l_t + 3)}}, \quad (\Delta v_t = \pm 1, -\Delta l_t = \pm 1, \Delta m_t = 0)
\end{aligned}

$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\langle v_t, l_t, m_t | q_t^{+1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(+i \chi_t) | v_t - 1, l_t - 1, m_t - 1 \rangle = \langle v_t - 1, l_t - 1, m_t - 1 | q_t^{-1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(-i \chi_t) | v_t, l_t, m_t \rangle\\[6pt]
&= + \sqrt{\frac{(v_t + l_t + 1)}{2}} \sqrt{\frac{(l_t + m_t)(l_t + m_t - 1)}{(2l_t - 1)(2l_t + 1)}}, \quad (\Delta v_t = \pm 1, \Delta l_t = \pm 1, \Delta m_t = \pm 1)
\end{aligned}

$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\langle v_t, l_t, m_t | q_t^{+1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(+i \chi_t) | v_t - 1, l_t + 1, m_t - 1 \rangle = \langle v_t - 1, l_t + 1, m_t - 1 | q_t^{-1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(-i \chi_t) | v_t, l_t, m_t \rangle\\[6pt]
&= + \sqrt{\frac{(v_t - l_t)}{2}} \sqrt{\frac{(l_t - m_t + 1)(l_t - m_t + 2)}{(2l_t + 1)(2l_t + 3)}}, \quad (\Delta v_t = \pm 1, -\Delta l_t = \pm 1, \Delta m_t = \pm 1)
\end{aligned}

$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\langle v_t, l_t, m_t | q_t^{-1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(+i \chi_t) | v_t - 1, l_t - 1, m_t + 1 \rangle = \langle v_t - 1, l_t - 1, m_t + 1 | q_t^{+1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(-i \chi_t) | v_t, l_t, m_t \rangle\\[6pt]
&= - \sqrt{\frac{(v_t + l_t + 1)}{2}} \sqrt{\frac{(l_t - m_t)(l_t - m_t - 1)}{(2l_t - 1)(2l_t + 1)}}, \quad (\Delta v_t = \pm 1, \Delta l_t = \pm 1, -\Delta m_t = \pm 1)
\end{aligned}

$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\langle v_t, l_t, m_t | q_t^{-1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(+i \chi_t) | v_t - 1, l_t + 1, m_t + 1 \rangle = \langle v_t - 1, l_t + 1, m_t + 1 | q_t^{+1} = r_t \sin \theta_t \exp(-i \chi_t) | v_t, l_t, m_t \rangle\\[6pt]
&= - \sqrt{\frac{(v_t - l_t)}{2}} \sqrt{\frac{(l_t + m_t + 1)(l_t + m_t + 2)}{(2l_t + 1)(2l_t + 3)}}, \quad (\Delta v_t = \pm 1, -\Delta l_t = \pm 1, -\Delta m_t = \pm 1)
\end{aligned}

$$

通过关系式 $q_{t1} = \frac{q_t^{+1} + q_t^{-1}}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad q_{t2} = \frac{q_t^{+1} - q_t^{-1}}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad q_{t3} = q_t^0$，可在分子固定（$abc$）坐标系中求得矩阵元素（即令 $q_{ta} = \frac{q_t^{+1} + q_t^{-1}}{\sqrt{2}}$、$q_{tb} = \frac{q_t^{+1} - q_t^{-1}}{\sqrt{2}}$、$q_{tc} = q_t^0$，此处不再进一步展开）。

此外，在置换反演群中还可能出现四重简并（如允许内部旋转的丙酮或二甲基乙炔）甚至更高重简并的情况。简并度为偶数时可用圆柱坐标系公式化，为奇数时则可用球面坐标系公式化。
