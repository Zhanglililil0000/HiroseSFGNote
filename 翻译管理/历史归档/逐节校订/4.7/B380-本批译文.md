#### 5d. 绕z轴扭转后再向后仰的CH₂基

分子面绕$z$轴扭转$\gamma$，同时从$xz$面起沿$\pm y$轴方向后仰角$\theta$时，由文件"欧拉角"的(Eu-3a)式可得：

$$\sin\chi = -1,\;\sin 2\chi = 0,\;\sin 3\chi = -1,\quad \cos\chi = 0,\;\cos 2\chi = -1,\;\cos 3\chi = 0$$

$$\sin\phi = \cos\gamma = \cos\tau/\sin\theta,\quad \sin 2\phi = \sin 2\gamma = 2\cos\tau\sqrt{\sin^2\theta - \cos^2\tau}/\sin^2\theta$$
$$\cos\phi = \sin\gamma = \sqrt{\sin^2\theta - \cos^2\tau}/\sin\theta,\quad \cos 2\phi = -\cos 2\gamma = (\sin^2\theta - 2\cos^2\tau)/\sin^2\theta$$
$$1 + \cos 2\phi = 1 - \cos 2\gamma = 2(\sin^2\theta - \cos^2\tau)/\sin^2\theta,\quad 1 + \cos\phi = 1 + \cos 2\gamma = 2\cos^2\tau/\sin^2\theta \tag{5d-1}$$

（$(\sin\gamma,\sin 2\gamma,\sin 3\gamma)$组或$(-\sin 2\gamma,-\sin 2\gamma,-\sin 3\gamma)$组；$(\sin\theta/-\sin\theta)$对；相邻CH₂基同为$+\gamma$或$-\gamma$，$\theta$的符号交替。）

由此得到：

**[对称伸缩振动]**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{xxx} &= -(1/2)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{bbc})\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin 2\phi \\
\chi_{xzz} &= (1/2)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{bbc})\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin 2\phi \\
\chi_{zxz} &= (1/2)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{bbc})\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin 2\phi \\
\chi_{xxz} &= (1/2)[\beta_{aac}(1 - \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 + \cos 2\phi)]\cos\theta \\
\chi_{zzz} &= (1/2)[\beta_{aac}(1 - \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 + \cos 2\phi)](\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) + \beta_{ccc}\cos^3\theta \\[6pt]
\chi_{yxx} &= (1/4)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)]\sin^3\theta + (1/2)\beta_{ccc}\sin^3\theta - (1/2)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{bbc})\sin\theta\cos 2\phi \\
\chi_{yzz} &= (1/2)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)](\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta) - \beta_{ccc}(\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta) \\
\chi_{yxz} &= (1/2)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{bbc})\cos^2\theta\sin 2\phi \\[6pt]
\chi_{yyx} &= (1/2)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{bbc})\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin 2\phi \\
\chi_{yyz} &= (1/2)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)]\cos^3\theta + \beta_{ccc}(\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{xyx} &= (1/4)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)]\sin^3\theta + (1/2)\beta_{ccc}\sin^3\theta - (1/2)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{bbc})\sin\theta\cos 2\phi \\
\chi_{zyz} &= (1/2)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)](\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta) - \beta_{ccc}(\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{yzy} &= -(1/2)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)](\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) + \beta_{ccc}(\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{xxy} &= -(1/4)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{bbc})(1 - \cos 2\phi) - (1/2)(\beta_{aac} + \beta_{bbc})\sin\theta + (1/2)(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta \\
\chi_{zzy} &= -(1/2)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)]\sin^3\theta - \beta_{ccc}(\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{zyy} &= -(1/2)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)](\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) + \beta_{ccc}(\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{yyy} &= (1/4)[\beta_{aac}(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \beta_{bbc}(1 - \cos 2\phi)]\sin^3\theta - (1/2)(\beta_{aac} + \beta_{bbc})\sin\theta - (1/2)\beta_{ccc}\sin^3\theta
\end{aligned}
$$

*(5d-2)*

**[反对称伸缩振动]**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{xxx} &= -\beta_{caa}\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin 2\phi \\
\chi_{zxx} &= \beta_{caa}\cos\theta \\
\chi_{xzx} &= \beta_{caa}\cos\theta \\[6pt]
\chi_{yzz} &= (1/2)\beta_{caa}[(\sin\theta - 2\sin^3\theta)(1 + \cos 2\phi) \\[6pt]
\chi_{yxy} &= (1/2)\beta_{caa}\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin 2\phi \\[6pt]
\chi_{xyy} &= (1/2)\beta_{caa}\sin\theta\cos\theta\sin 2\phi \\[6pt]
\chi_{yyy} &= -(1/2)\beta_{caa}[(\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta)(1 + \cos 2\phi) + \sin\theta(1 - \cos 2\phi)]
\end{aligned}
$$
