#### 5b. 后仰的CH₂基

分子面从$xz$面起沿$\pm y$轴方向后仰角$\theta$以消除立体障碍时，由文件"欧拉角"的(Eu-2a)式可得：

$$
\begin{gathered}
R_z(\chi = -\pi/2)\;R_{b'}(-\theta)\;R_c(\phi = \pi/2),\\
R_z(\chi = -\pi/2)\;R_{b'}(\theta)\;R_c(\phi = \pi/2),\\
\tau = \pi/2 - \theta
\end{gathered}
$$

$$\sin\chi = -1,\;\sin 2\chi = 0,\;\sin 3\chi = +1,\quad \cos\chi = 0,\;\cos 2\chi = -1,\;\cos 3\chi = 0$$
$$\sin\phi = +1,\;\sin 2\phi = 0,\;\sin 3\phi = -1,\quad \cos\phi = 0,\;\cos 2\phi = -1,\;\cos 3\phi = 0$$
$$\sin\theta = -(\pm)\cos\tau,\quad \cos\theta = \sin\tau \tag{5b-1}$$

（$(\sin\theta/-\sin\theta)$对；相邻CH₂基交替取$+\theta$与$-\theta$。）

由此得到：

**[对称伸缩振动]**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{xxz} &= \beta_{aac}\cos\theta \\
\chi_{zzz} &= \beta_{bbc}(\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) + \beta_{ccc}\cos^3\theta \\[6pt]
\chi_{yzz} &= (\beta_{bbc} - \beta_{ccc})(\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{yyz} &= (\beta_{bbc} - \beta_{ccc})\cos^3\theta + \beta_{ccc}\cos\theta \\[6pt]
\chi_{zyz} &= (\beta_{bbc} - \beta_{ccc})(\sin\theta - \sin^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{yzy} &= -(\beta_{bbc} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{xxy} &= -\beta_{aac}\sin\theta \\
\chi_{zzy} &= -(\beta_{bbc} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta - \beta_{ccc}\sin\theta \\[6pt]
\chi_{zyy} &= -(\beta_{bbc} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos\theta - \cos^3\theta) \\[6pt]
\chi_{yyy} &= -\beta_{bbc}\sin\theta + (\beta_{bbc} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta
\end{aligned}
$$

*(5b-2)*

**[反对称伸缩振动]**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{zxx} &= \beta_{caa}\cos\theta \\
\chi_{xzx} &= \beta_{caa}\cos\theta \\[6pt]
\chi_{yxx} &= -\beta_{caa}\sin\theta \\[6pt]
\chi_{xyx} &= -\beta_{caa}\sin\theta
\end{aligned}
$$
