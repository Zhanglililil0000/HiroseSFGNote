### 4a. 一般式

先给出一般表达式。它们是按取向条件进行简化时的出发点，也应能适用于各种条件。

#### 全对称振动

CH基团的和频张量表达式也与下面相同。在$\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}\gg\beta_{\xi\xi\zeta},\beta_{\eta\eta\zeta}$时，$\beta_{aac}\sim(4/9)\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}$、$\beta_{ccc}\sim(1/9)\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}$。

**(ppp)**


$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{xxx} &= \frac{1}{4}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta(3\cos \chi + \cos 3\chi) - \beta_{aac}\sin \theta \cos \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{xzz} &= (\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\sin \theta - \sin^3\theta)\cos \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{zxz} &= (\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\sin \theta - \sin^3\theta)\cos \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{zzx} &= (\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\sin \theta - \sin^3\theta)\cos \chi - \beta_{aac}\sin \theta \cos \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{xzx} &= -\frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)(1 + \cos 2\chi) \\[4pt]
\chi_{zxx} &= -\frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)(1 + \cos 2\chi) \\[4pt]
\chi_{xxz} &= -\frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)(1 + \cos 2\chi) + \beta_{aac}\cos \theta \\[4pt]
\chi_{zzz} &= -(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\cos^3\theta + \beta_{aac}\cos \theta
\end{aligned}
$$


**(spp)**


$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{yxx} &= -\frac{1}{4}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta(\sin \chi + \sin 3\chi) \\[4pt]
\chi_{yzz} &= -(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\sin \theta - \sin^3\theta)\sin \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{yzx} &= \frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)\sin 2\chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{yxz} &= \frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)\sin 2\chi
\end{aligned}
$$


**(ssp)**


$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{yyx} &= \frac{1}{4}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta(\cos \chi - \cos 3\chi) - \beta_{aac}\sin \theta \cos \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{yyz} &= -\frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)(1 - \cos 2\chi) + \beta_{aac}\cos \theta
\end{aligned}
$$


**(psp)**


$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{xyx} &= -\frac{1}{4}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta(\sin \chi + \sin 3\chi) \\[4pt]
\chi_{zyz} &= -(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\sin \theta - \sin^3\theta)\sin \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{xyz} &= \frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)\sin 2\chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{zyx} &= \frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)\sin 2\chi
\end{aligned}
$$


**(sps)**


$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{yxy} &= \frac{1}{4}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta(\cos \chi - \cos 3\chi) \\[4pt]
\chi_{yzy} &= -\frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)(1 - \cos 2\chi)
\end{aligned}
$$


**(pps)**


$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{xxy} &= -\frac{1}{4}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta(\sin \chi + \sin 3\chi) + \beta_{aac}\sin \theta \sin \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{zzy} &= -(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\sin \theta - \sin^3\theta)\sin \chi + \beta_{aac}\sin \theta \sin \chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{xzy} &= \frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)\sin 2\chi \\[4pt]
\chi_{zxy} &= \frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)\sin 2\chi
\end{aligned}
$$


**(pss)**


$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi_{xyy} &= \frac{1}{4}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta(\cos \chi - \cos 3\chi) \\[4pt]
\chi_{zyy} &= -\frac{1}{2}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})(\cos \theta - \cos^3\theta)(1 - \cos 2\chi)
\end{aligned}
$$


**(sss)**


$$
\chi_{yyy} = -\frac{1}{4}(\beta_{aac} - \beta_{ccc})\sin^3\theta(3\sin \chi - \sin 3\chi) + \beta_{aac}\sin \theta \sin \chi
