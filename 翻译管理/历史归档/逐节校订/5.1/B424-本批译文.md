## 线性极化与不可约基底

电场分量$E_j$与位置矢量一样，属于极矢量，也是单指标张量的基底$V_b\,(V_x)$、$V_a\,(V_y)$、$V_0\,(V_z)$。另一方面，分子极化率张量$\boldsymbol\alpha$是二指标张量；其笛卡尔坐标分量$\alpha_{ij}$可表示为V群基底的线性组合，这些基底包括0阶分量$\alpha_0^{(0)}$、1阶分量$\alpha_b^{(1)}$、$\alpha_a^{(1)}$、$\alpha_0^{(1)}$，以及2阶分量$\alpha_{2b}^{(2)}$、$\alpha_{2a}^{(2)}$、$\alpha_{1b}^{(2)}$、$\alpha_{1a}^{(2)}$、$\alpha_0^{(2)}$。实际上，线性极化也可用下式表示：

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_x={}&\left(\frac{\alpha_0^{(0)}}{\sqrt3}
-\frac{\alpha_0^{(2)}}{\sqrt6}
+\frac{\alpha_{2b}^{(2)}}{\sqrt2}\right)E_x\\
&+\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(\alpha_0^{(1)}+\alpha_{2a}^{(2)}\right)E_y
+\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(\alpha_{1a}^{(1)}+\alpha_{1b}^{(2)}\right)E_z.
\end{aligned}\tag{1a}
$$

> **原文疑点（Q200，PDF p2，式(1a)的$E_z$项）：** 底本为$\bigl(\alpha_{1a}^{(1)}+\alpha_{1b}^{(2)}\bigr)/\sqrt2$。按下方式(2)的定义代入，此组合等于$\alpha_{zx}$，而本节前页的$P_i=\sum_j\alpha_{ij}E_j$要求此处系数为$\alpha_{xz}$。候选为$\bigl(-\alpha_{1a}^{(1)}+\alpha_{1b}^{(2)}\bigr)/\sqrt2$；正文保留原式，候选尚未采用。若另有限定$\alpha_{xz}=\alpha_{zx}$，该差异可消失，但本段未明确作此限定。

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_y={}&\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(-\alpha_0^{(1)}+\alpha_{2a}^{(2)}\right)E_x\\
&+\left(\frac{\alpha_0^{(0)}}{\sqrt3}
-\frac{\alpha_0^{(2)}}{\sqrt6}
-\frac{\alpha_{2b}^{(2)}}{\sqrt2}\right)E_y\\
&+\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(\alpha_{1b}^{(1)}+\alpha_{1a}^{(2)}\right)E_z.
\end{aligned}\tag{1b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_z={}&\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(\alpha_{1a}^{(1)}+\alpha_{1b}^{(2)}\right)E_x
+\frac1{\sqrt2}\left(-\alpha_{1b}^{(1)}+\alpha_{1a}^{(2)}\right)E_y\\
&+\left(\frac{\alpha_0^{(0)}}{\sqrt3}
+\frac{2\alpha_0^{(2)}}{\sqrt6}\right)E_z.
\end{aligned}\tag{1c}
$$

上述表达式使用了基底张量与张量笛卡尔坐标分量之间的下列关系：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_0^{(0)}&=\frac{\alpha_{xx}+\alpha_{yy}+\alpha_{zz}}{\sqrt3},\\
\alpha_{1b}^{(1)}&=\frac{\alpha_{yz}-\alpha_{zy}}{\sqrt2},\\
\alpha_{1a}^{(1)}&=\frac{\alpha_{zx}-\alpha_{xz}}{\sqrt2},\\
\alpha_0^{(1)}&=\frac{\alpha_{xy}-\alpha_{yx}}{\sqrt2},\\
\alpha_{2b}^{(2)}&=\frac{\alpha_{xx}-\alpha_{yy}}{\sqrt2},\\
\alpha_{2a}^{(2)}&=\frac{\alpha_{xy}+\alpha_{yx}}{\sqrt2},\\
\alpha_{1b}^{(2)}&=\frac{\alpha_{zx}+\alpha_{xz}}{\sqrt2},\\
\alpha_{1a}^{(2)}&=\frac{\alpha_{yz}+\alpha_{zy}}{\sqrt2},\\
\alpha_0^{(2)}&=\frac{-\alpha_{xx}-\alpha_{yy}+2\alpha_{zz}}{\sqrt6}.
\end{aligned}\tag{2}
$$

> **记号说明（译者）：** 本页引言将1阶的两个分量写为$\alpha_b^{(1)}$、$\alpha_a^{(1)}$，式(1)、式(2)则写为$\alpha_{1b}^{(1)}$、$\alpha_{1a}^{(1)}$；这里分别保留原文写法。$V_b$、$V_a$与$V_x$、$V_y$的对应也按本页原文保留。
