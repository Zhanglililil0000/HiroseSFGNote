### 式(5a)：首指标为x的分量

> **排版说明（译者）：** 原式(5a)跨PDF p3—4。以下按原顺序逐分量断行，式(5a)—(5c)的每组9个等式共用原组号；分段不改变原指标、项序或符号。

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xxx}={}&\frac{2Y^{(1)}_{1b,1}}{\sqrt{8}}+\frac{2Y^{(1)}_{1b,3}}{\sqrt{40}}-\frac{3Y^{(3)}_{1b}}{\sqrt{60}}\\
&-\frac{Y^{(3)}_{3b}}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xyy}={}&\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1b,1}}{\sqrt{8}}+\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1b,2}}{2}-\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1b,3}}{\sqrt{40}}\\
&+\frac{Y^{(2)}_{1a,1}}{2}-\frac{Y^{(2)}_{1a,2}}{\sqrt{12}}-\frac{Y^{(3)}_{1b}}{\sqrt{60}}\\
&+\frac{Y^{(3)}_{3b}}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xzz}={}&\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1b,1}}{\sqrt{8}}+\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1b,2}}{2}-\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1b,3}}{\sqrt{40}}\\
&-\frac{Y^{(2)}_{1a,1}}{2}+\frac{Y^{(2)}_{1a,2}}{\sqrt{12}}+\frac{4Y^{(3)}_{1b}}{\sqrt{60}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xxy}={}&\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1a,1}}{\sqrt{8}}-\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1a,2}}{2}-\frac{Y^{(1)}_{1a,3}}{\sqrt{40}}\\
&+\frac{Y^{(2)}_{1b,1}}{2}+\frac{Y^{(2)}_{1b,2}}{\sqrt{12}}-\frac{Y^{(3)}_{1a}}{\sqrt{60}}\\
&+\frac{Y^{(3)}_{3a}}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xyx}={}&\frac{4Y^{(1)}_{1a,3}}{\sqrt{40}}-\frac{2Y^{(2)}_{1b,2}}{\sqrt{12}}-\frac{Y^{(3)}_{1a}}{\sqrt{60}}\\
&+\frac{Y^{(3)}_{3a}}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xyz}={}&\frac{Y^{(0)}_{0}}{\sqrt{6}}+\frac{Y^{(2)}_{0,1}}{\sqrt{12}}-\frac{Y^{(2)}_{0,2}}{2}\\
&+\frac{Y^{(2)}_{02b,1}}{2}+\frac{Y^{(2)}_{2b,2}}{\sqrt{12}}+\frac{Y^{(3)}_{2a}}{\sqrt{6}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xzy}={}&-\frac{Y^{(0)}_{0}}{\sqrt{6}}+\frac{2Y^{(2)}_{0,1}}{\sqrt{12}}-\frac{2Y^{(2)}_{2b,2}}{\sqrt{12}}\\
&+\frac{Y^{(3)}_{2a}}{\sqrt{6}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xzx}={}&\frac{4Y^{(1)}_{0,3}}{\sqrt{40}}+\frac{2Y^{(2)}_{2a,2}}{\sqrt{12}}+\frac{Y^{(3)}_{0}}{\sqrt{10}}\\
&-\frac{Y^{(3)}_{2b}}{\sqrt{6}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta_{xxz}={}&\frac{Y^{(1)}_{0,1}}{\sqrt{8}}-\frac{Y^{(1)}_{0,2}}{2}-\frac{Y^{(1)}_{0,3}}{\sqrt{40}}\\
&-\frac{Y^{(2)}_{2a,1}}{2}-\frac{Y^{(2)}_{2a,2}}{\sqrt{12}}-\frac{Y^{(3)}_{0}}{\sqrt{10}}\\
&-\frac{Y^{(3)}_{2b}}{\sqrt{6}}
\end{aligned}\tag{5a}
$$

> **原文疑点（Q203，PDF p3—4，式(5)）：** $Y_{02b,1}^{(2)}$中的前导0在xyz、yxz、zxy、zyx四行均按底本保留；与其他基底的$2b,1$记法是否相同尚未确认，不自行删去。另，xxy及xyx两行均按底本保留各项系数，未按对称关系替换。
