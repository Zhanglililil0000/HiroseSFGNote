## 准备工作

原点取在光线入射点；光束有宽度时取其中央。$z$ 轴沿介质1指向介质2的法线；$x$ 轴沿光在界面上的传播方向，与波矢在界面上的投影一致。

| 记号 | 定义 |
|---|---|
| $1/m$ 界面 | 介质1与薄膜的界面 |
| $m/2$ 界面 | 介质1与薄膜的界面（底本原称） |
| $n_1,n_2,n_m$ | 介质1、介质2与膜 $m$ 的折射率 |

### 反射系数

$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{12}^s&=E_r^s/E_i^s=(k_{1z}-k_{2z})/(k_{1z}+k_{2z})\\
&=(n_1\cos\theta_1-n_2\cos\theta_2)/(n_1\cos\theta_1+n_2\cos\theta_2)\\
&=-\sin(\theta_1-\theta_2)/\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2),\\
r_{12}^p&=E_r^p/E_i^p=(\varepsilon_1k_{2z}-\varepsilon_2k_{1z})/(\varepsilon_2k_{1z}+\varepsilon_1k_{2z})\\
&=(n_1\cos\theta_2-n_2\cos\theta_1)/(n_2\cos\theta_1+n_1\cos\theta_2)\\
&=-\tan(\theta_1-\theta_2)/\tan(\theta_1+\theta_2).
\end{aligned}
$$

### 透射系数

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_{12}^s&=E_t^s/E_i^s=2k_{1z}/(k_{1z}+k_{2z})\\
&=2n_1\cos\theta_1/(n_1\cos\theta_1+n_2\cos\theta_2)\\
&=2\cos\theta_1\sin\theta_2/\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2),\\
t_{12}^p&=E_t^p/E_i^p=2\varepsilon_1k_{2z}\cos\theta_1/(\varepsilon_2k_{1z}+\varepsilon_1k_{2z})\\
&=2n_1\cos\theta_1/(n_2\cos\theta_1+n_1\cos\theta_2)\\
&=2\cos\theta_1\sin\theta_2/\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2)\cos(\theta_1-\theta_2).
\end{aligned}
$$

反向入射时，有：

$$
r_{21}^s=-r_{12}^s,\qquad r_{21}^p=-r_{12}^p
$$

$$
t_{21}^s=(n_2\cos\theta_2/n_1\cos\theta_1)t_{12}^s,\qquad t_{21}^p=(n_2\cos\theta_2/n_1\cos\theta_1)t_{12}^p
$$

以界面内向法线为 $z$ 轴，以s偏振电场方向为 $y$ 轴，取光沿界面的传播方向为 $x$ 正向，可得电场的轴向分量：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{x,i}&=E_i^p\cos\theta_1,\quad E_{y,i}=E_{ri}^s,\quad E_{z,i}=-E_i^p\sin\theta_1,\\
E_{x,r}&=E_r^p\cos\theta_1,\quad E_{y,r}=E_r^s,\quad E_{z,r}=E_r^p\sin\theta_1,\\
E_{x,t}&=E_t^p\cos\theta_2,\quad E_{y,t}=E_t^s,\quad E_{z,t}=-E_t^p\sin\theta_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

由上式，得到下列振幅反射系数与“振幅等效系数”的表达式：

$$
r_{12}^x=r_{12}^p,\quad r_{12}^y=r_{12}^s,\quad r_{12}^z=-r_{12}^p
$$

$$
r_{21}^x=-r_{12}^p,\quad r_{21}^y=-r_{12}^s,\quad r_{21}^z=r_{12}^p
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_{12}^x&=(\cos\theta_2/\cos\theta_1)t_{12}^p,\quad t_{12}^y=t_{12}^s,\\
t_{12}^z&=(\sin\theta_2/\sin\theta_1)t_{12}^p=(n_1/n_2)t_{12}^p
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
t_{21}^x=(n_2/n_1)t_{12}^p,\quad t_{12}^y=t_{12}^s,\quad t_{12}^z=(\cos\theta_2/\cos\theta_1)(n_2/n_1)^2t_{12}^p
$$

> **译校注（Q078及关联第2.2节Q072）：** PDF p2原文写「振幅等価係数」，这里照译“振幅等效系数”；按所列 $t$ 系数，候选为“振幅透射系数”，未改原词。轴分量首行原为 $E^s_{ri}$；反向透射一行先写 $t^x_{21}$，随后却写 $t^y_{12},t^z_{12}$。这些记号依本独立原页保留，不按对称性统一。

对于两侧界面，有：

$$
\begin{aligned}
r_{1m,x}&=-r_{m1,x}=r_{1m,p},\quad r_{1m,y}=-r_{m1,y}=r_{1m,s},\quad r_{1m,z}=-r_{m1,z}=-r_{1m,p}\\
r_{2m,x}&=-r_{m2,x}=r_{2m,p},\quad r_{2m,y}=-r_{m2,y}=r_{2m,s},\quad r_{2m,z}=-r_{m2,z}=-r_{2m,p}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_{1m,x}&=(\cos\theta_m/\cos\theta_1)t_{1m,p},\quad t_{1m,y}=t_{1m,s},\\
t_{1m,z}&=(\sin\theta_m/\sin\theta_1)t_{1m,p}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_{m1,x}&=(\cos\theta_1/\cos\theta_m)t_{m1,p},\quad t_{m1,y}=t_{m1,s},\\
t_{m1,z}&=(\sin\theta_1/\sin\theta_m)t_{m1,p}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_{2m,x}&=(\cos\theta_m/\cos\theta_2)t_{2m,p},\quad t_{2m,y}=t_{2m,s},\\
t_{2m,z}&=(\sin\theta_m/\sin\theta_2)t_{2m,p}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_{m2,x}&=(\cos\theta_2/\cos\theta_m)t_{m2,p},\quad t_{m2,y}=t_{m2,s},\\
t_{m2,z}&=(\sin\theta_2/\sin\theta_m)t_{m2,p}.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_{1m,\alpha}t_{m1,\alpha}&=1+r_{1m,\alpha}r_{m1,\alpha}=1-r_{1m,\alpha}^2,\\
t_{2m,\alpha}t_{m2,\alpha}&=1+r_{2m,\alpha}r_{m2,\alpha}=1-r_{2m,\alpha}^2,\qquad(\alpha=x,y,z).
\end{aligned}
$$

上式满足以下关系；首行表示电场的边界条件：

$$
1+r_{12}^x=t_{12}^x,\qquad 1+r_{12}^y=t_{12}^y,\qquad
1+r_{12}^z=(n_2/n_1)^2t_{12}^z.
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
1+r_{12}^sr_{21}^s&=1-(r_{12}^s)^2=t_{12}^st_{21}^s,\\
1+r_{12}^pr_{21}^p&=1-(r_{12}^p)^2=t_{12}^pt_{21}^p.
\end{aligned}
$$

光强正比于 $E^2n\cos\theta$，因此 $r_{12}^2n_1\cos\theta_1+t_{12}^2n_2\cos\theta_2=n_1\cos\theta_1$，即光强守恒。

> **译校注（关联第2.2节Q053，适用范围）：** 上述普通平方与守恒式照录底本。若振幅为复数，需核对模平方及吸收条件，不将这一简式无条件用于吸收介质；这里未据候选重写原关系。末行的 $\alpha$ 只是 $x,y,z$ 的坐标分量枚举下标，不是分子极化率。

## L系数与电场振幅

$$
E_SF, \alpha = \sum_\beta L_\alpha\beta P_\beta^SF
$$

此处省略了公共因子 $4\pi i \omega_{SF}/c$（当使用波数矢量代替折射率时，则为 $4\pi i \omega_{SF}^2/c^2$）。

L 系数的记号规则：Li/j, s(p), $\alpha$ 表示当极化片 m' 夹在 i 层与 j 层之间时，由极化强度的 $\alpha$ 分量（$\alpha = x, y, z$）产生的光的 s 偏振分量或 p 偏振分量之间的系数（注意：并非 x、y、z 分量）。向上(-)光与向下(+)光分别以上标 -、+ 区分。

对于媒质1与多层薄膜 m 之间的极化片 m' 产生的光：

$$
L^-_1/m,p,x = cos\thetam,SF / (n1,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta1,SF)

L^-_1/m,s,y = 1 / (n1,SF cos\theta1,SF + nm,SF cos\thetam,SF)

L^-_1/m,p,z = (nm,SF / nm',SF) sin\thetam',SF / (n1,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta1,SF)
           = (nm,SF / nm',SF)^2 sin\thetam,SF / (n1,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta1,SF)

L^+_1/m,p,x = cos\theta1,SF / (n1,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta1,SF)

L^+_1/m,s,y = 1 / (n1,SF cos\theta1,SF + nm,SF cos\thetam,SF)

L^+_1/m,p,z = -(n1,SF / nm',SF) sin\thetam',SF / (n1,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta1,SF)
           = -(n1,SF / nm',SF)^2 sin\theta1,SF / (n1,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta1,SF)
$$

对于媒质2与多层薄膜 m 之间的极化片 m'' 产生的光：

$$
L^-_2/m,p,x = cos\theta2,SF / (n2,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta2,SF)

L^-_2/m,s,y = 1 / (n2,SF cos\theta2,SF + nm,SF cos\thetam,SF)

L^-_2/m,p,z = (n2,SF / nm'',SF) sin\thetam'',SF / (n2,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta2,SF)
           = (n2,SF / nm'',SF)^2 sin\theta2,SF / (n2,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta2,SF)

L^+_2/m,p,x = cos\thetam,SF / (n2,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta2,SF)

L^+_2/m,s,y = 1 / (n2,SF cos\theta2,SF + nm,SF cos\thetam,SF)

L^+_2/m,p,z = -(nm,SF / nm'',SF) sin\thetam'',SF / (n2,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta2,SF)
           = -(nm,SF / nm'',SF)^2 sin\thetam,SF / (n2,SF cos\thetam,SF + nm,SF cos\theta2,SF)
$$

对于多层薄膜内部的极化片产生的光：

$$
L^-_m/m,p,x = L^+_m/m,p,x = cos\thetam,SF / (2 nm,SF cos\thetam,SF)

L^-_m/m,s,y = L^+_m/m,s,y = 1 / (2 nm,SF cos\thetam,SF)

L^-_m/m,p,z = -L^+_m/m,p,z = sin\thetam,SF / (2 nm,SF cos\thetam,SF)
$$

由此，各部位产生的 SFG 光电场振幅可表示为：

**来自 P(0) 的贡献**：（P(0^-) 是媒质1侧电场引起的极化强度，P(0^+) 是薄膜侧电场引起的极化强度）

$$
E^-_1,p(z = 0^-) = L^-_1/m,p,x P_x(0^-) + L^-_1/m,p,z P_z(0^-)

E^-_1,s(z = 0^-) = L^-_1/m,s,y P_y(0^-)

E^+_m,p(z = 0^+) = L^+_1/m,p,x P_x(0^+) + L^+_1/m,p,z P_z(0^+)

E^+_m,s(z = 0^+) = L^+_1/m,s,y P_y(0^+)

**来自 P(hm) 的贡献**：（P(hm^+) 是媒质2侧电场引起的极化强度，P(hm^-) 是薄膜侧电场引起的极化强度）

E^-_m,p(z = hm^-) = L^-_2/m,p,x P_x(hm^-) + L^-_2/m,p,z P_z(hm^-)

E^-_m,s(z = hm^-) = L^-_2/m,s,y P_y(hm^-)

E^+_2,p(z = hm^+) = L^+_2/m,p,x P_x(hm^+) + L^+_2/m,p,z P_z(hm^+)

E^+_2,s(z = hm^+) = L^+_2/m,s,y P_y(hm^+)

**来自 P(z) 的贡献**：

E^-_m,p(z) = L^-_m/m,p,x P_x(z) + L^-_m/m,p,z P_z(z)

E^-_m,s(z) = L^-_m/m,s,y P_y(z)

E^+_m,p(z) = L^+_m/m,p,x P_x(z) + L^+_m/m,p,z P_z(z)

E^+_m,s(z) = L^+_m/m,s,y P_y(z)
$$

## 1/m 界面的外侧 (x = -2nh_m tan\thetam,SF, z = 0^-) 的 SFG

### 反射 SFG

$$
E^-_1p(0-) = E^0_vis,\beta E^0_ir,\gamma \sum_\beta\gamma
    (1 + r1m,vis,\beta)(1 + rm2,vis,\beta e^{2i\betam,vis hm}) (1 + r1m,ir,\gamma)(1 + rm2,ir,\gamma e^{2i\betam,ir hm})
  / [(1 + r1m,vis,\beta rm2,vis,\beta e^{2i\betam,vis hm}) (1 + r1m,ir,\gamma rm2,ir,\gamma e^{2i\betam,ir hm})]
  * [(1 + rm2,SF,p e^{2i\betam,SF hm}) L^-_1/m,px \chi_x\beta\gamma + (1 - rm2,SF,p e^{2i\betam,SF hm}) L^-_1/m,pz \chi_z\beta\gamma]
  / [1 + r1m,SF,p rm2,SF,p e^{2i\betam,SF hm}]
  ... (1a)

E^-_1s(0-) = E^0_vis,\beta E^0_ir,\gamma \sum_\beta\gamma
    (1 + r1m,vis,\beta)(1 + rm2,vis,\beta e^{2i\betam,vis hm}) (1 + r1m,ir,\gamma)(1 + rm2,ir,\gamma e^{2i\betam,ir hm})
  / [(1 + r1m,vis,\beta rm2,vis,\beta e^{2i\betam,vis hm}) (1 + r1m,ir,\gamma rm2,ir,\gamma e^{2i\betam,ir hm})]
  * [(1 + rm2,SF,s e^{2i\betam,SF hm}) / (1 + r1m,SF,s rm2,SF,s e^{2i\betam,SF hm})] L^-_1/m,sy \chi_y\beta\gamma
  ... (1b)
$$

  ... (2a)

  ... (2b)
$$

## 表面已形成薄膜时（折射率 m' 的分子膜）的 SFG

当表面已形成完整薄膜时，由构成该薄膜（折射率 m'）的分子产生的 SFG 需要考虑内部反射。

### 反射 SFG

$$
E^-_1p(0-) = E^0_vis,\beta E^0_ir,\gamma \sum_\beta,\gamma
    t1m',vis,\beta (1 + rm'm,vis,\beta)(1 + rm2,vis,\beta e^{2i\betam,vis hm})

E^-_1s(0-) = E^0_vis,\beta E^0_ir,\gamma \sum_\beta,\gamma

  * t1m',ir,\gamma (1 + rm'm,ir,\gamma)(1 + rm2,ir,\gamma e^{2i\betam,ir hm})
  / [1 + r1m',ir,\gamma rm'm,ir,\gamma + (rm'm,ir,\gamma + r1m',ir,\gamma) rm2,ir,\gamma e^{2i\betam,ir hm}]

  / [1 + r1m',SF,s rm'm,SF,s + rm2,SF,s (r1m',SF,s + rm'm,SF,s) e^{2i\betam,SF hm}] \chi_y\beta\gamma L^-_1/m,sy
  ... (3b)
$$

### 透射 SFG

$$
E^+_2p(hm+) = E^0_vis,\beta E^0_ir,\gamma \sum_\beta,\gamma
    t1m',vis,\beta (1 + rm'm,vis,\beta)(1 + rm2,vis,\beta e^{2i\betam,vis hm})
  / [1 + r1m',vis,\beta rm'm,vis,\beta + (rm'm,vis,\beta + r1m',vis,\beta) rm2,vis,\beta e^{2i\betam,vis hm}]
  * t1m',ir,\gamma (1 + rm'm,ir,\gamma)(1 + rm2,ir,\gamma e^{2i\betam,ir hm})
  / [1 + r1m',ir,\gamma rm'm,ir,\gamma + (rm'm,ir,\gamma + r1m',ir,\gamma) rm2,ir,\gamma e^{2i\betam,ir hm}]
  * tm2,SF,p (1 + r1m',SF,p rm'm,SF,p) e^{i\betam,SF hm}
  / [1 + r1m',SF,p rm'm,SF,p + rm2,SF,p (rmm',SF,p + rm'1,SF,p) e^{2i\betam,SF hm}] (L^+_1/m,px \chi_x\beta\gamma + L^+_1/m,pz \chi_z\beta\gamma)
