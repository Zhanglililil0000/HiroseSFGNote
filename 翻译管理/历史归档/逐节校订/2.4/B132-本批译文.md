## 膜体相产生的和频光

体相范围与源点位置为：


$$
\begin{aligned}
0&<z<h_m, \\
A_{n=0,1,2}:\quad x&=-2nh_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}-z\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}, \\
B_{n=-1,0,1,2}:\quad x&=-(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+z\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

> **记号说明（译者）**：这里的 $\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF/vis/ir}}$ 是传播量；求和下标 $\beta,\gamma$ 则枚举入射场与二阶非线性极化率的分量，两种用途按原文保留。原文前因子为 $i/\delta$，本独立文件未在此定义 $\delta$，不以旧译 $i\Delta$ 代换，也不补其含义。

### 反射和频光


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1p}(0^-)&=\frac{i}{\delta}\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})}\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{m1,\mathrm{SF},p}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times\big\{\frac{1}{\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}-\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}-\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}}\big\{\chi_{x\beta\gamma}L^{-}_{1/m,p,x}[r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m})\\
&\qquad+r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})] \\
&\qquad+\chi_{z\beta\gamma}L^{-}_{1/m,p,z}[r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m})\\
&\qquad-r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})]\big\} \\
&\quad+\frac{1}{\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}-\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}}\big\{\chi_{x\beta\gamma}L^{-}_{1/m,p,x}[r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})\\
&\qquad+r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m})] \\
&\qquad+\chi_{z\beta\gamma}L^{-}_{1/m,p,z}[r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})\\
&\qquad-r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m})]\big\} \\
&\quad+\frac{1}{\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}-\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}}\big\{\chi_{x\beta\gamma}L^{-}_{m/m,p,x}[r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})\\
&\qquad+r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m})] \\
&\qquad+\chi_{z\beta\gamma}L^{-}_{m/m,p,z}[r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})\\
&\qquad-r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m})]\big\} \\
&\quad+\frac{1}{\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}}\big\{\chi_{x\beta\gamma}L^{-}_{m/m,p,x}[(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-1)\\
&\qquad+r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m})] \\
&\qquad+\chi_{z\beta\gamma}L^{-}_{m/m,p,z}[(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-1)\\
&\qquad-r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m}-e^{2i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}})h_m})]\big\}
\end{aligned}\tag{13a}
$$
