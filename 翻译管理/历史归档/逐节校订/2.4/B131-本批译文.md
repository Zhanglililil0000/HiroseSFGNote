## m/2界面内侧产生的反射和频光

源点为 $x=-(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}$、$z=h_m^-$。

> **标题说明（译者）**：原文总标题含“反射”，其下仍依次列反射与透射两组，此处按原顺序保留。

### 反射和频光


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1p}(0^-)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{m1,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})}(L^{-}_{2/m,p,x}\chi_{x\beta\gamma}+L^{-}_{2/m,p,z}\chi_{z\beta\gamma})
\end{aligned}\tag{11a}
$$


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1s}(0^-)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{m1,\mathrm{SF},s}e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},s}r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},s}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})}L^{-}_{2/m,s,y}\chi_{y\beta\gamma}
\end{aligned}\tag{11b}
$$

### 透射和频光


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{2p}(h_m^+)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{(1+r_{m1,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})L^{+}_{2/m,p,x}\chi_{x\beta\gamma}+(-1+r_{m1,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})L^{+}_{2/m,p,z}\chi_{z\beta\gamma}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})}
\end{aligned}\tag{12a}
$$


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{2s}(h_m^+)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{(1+r_{m1,\mathrm{SF},s}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})L^{+}_{2/m,s,y}\chi_{y\beta\gamma}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},s}r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},s}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})}
\end{aligned}\tag{12b}
$$

> **原文疑点（Q083，PDF p7—8，式12a、12b）**：求和中的两入射场之后有孤立的 $e$，未见指数。此处照录；删除它只是候选。式12a的 $z$ 项写作 $-1+r_{m1,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}$，未按相邻式改号。
