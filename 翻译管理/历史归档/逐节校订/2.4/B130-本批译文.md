## m/2界面外侧产生的和频光

源点为 $x=-(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}$、$z=h_m^+$。

### 反射和频光


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1p}(0^-)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}t_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,wvis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{m1,p}e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}}{1+r_{1m,p}r_{m2,p}\,2e^{2\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}}(L^{-}_{2/m,p,x}\chi_{x\beta\gamma}+L^{-}_{2/m,p,z}\chi_{z\beta\gamma})
\end{aligned}\tag{7a}
$$


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1s}(0^-)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}t_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,wvis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{m1,s}e^{\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}}{1+r_{1m,s}r_{m2,s}\,2e^{2\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}}L^{-}_{2/m,s,y}\chi_{y\beta\gamma}
\end{aligned}\tag{7b}
$$

> **原文疑点（Q082，PDF p6，式7a、7b）**：激发光分母的可见光反射系数原写 $r_{m2,wvis,\beta}$。和频出射分母在 $r_{m2,p(s)}$ 后另有基线数字2，随后指数写 $2\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m$，未见 $i$；7b出射分子的指数也未见 $i$。这里保留这些写法以及省略SF的系数下标；候选为常规 $1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},p(s)}r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p(s)}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}$ 及相应出射相位，未采用。

### 透射和频光


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{2p}(h_m^+)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}t_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,wvis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{(1-r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})L^{+}_{2/m,p,x}\chi_{x\beta\gamma}+(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})L^{+}_{2/m,p,z}\chi_{z\beta\gamma}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})}
\end{aligned}\tag{8a}
$$


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{2s}(h_m^+)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}t_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m2,wvis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m})(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m})} \\
&\quad\times \frac{(1-r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},s}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})L^{+}_{2/m,s,y}\chi_{y\beta\gamma}}{(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},s}r_{m2,\mathrm{SF},s}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m})}
\end{aligned}\tag{8b}
$$

> **原文疑点（Q082续）**：式8a、8b同样使用 $wvis$，也按原文保留。

### 发出和频光的界面为薄膜时

当发出和频光的界面为折射率以 $m^{\prime\prime}$ 标示的薄膜时，有：

#### 反射和频光


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1p}(0^-)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{vis},\beta}(1+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}}{(1+r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}(r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{vis},\beta}+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}t_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(1+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}}{(1+r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{ir},\gamma}+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{m1,\mathrm{SF},p}e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}(1+r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{SF},p})}{1+r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{SF},p}r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{SF},p}+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},p}(r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{SF},p}+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{SF},p})e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}}(L^{-}_{2/m,p,x}\chi_{x\beta\gamma}+L^{-}_{2/m,p,z}\chi_{z\beta\gamma})
\end{aligned}\tag{9a}
$$


$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1s}(0^-)&=\sum_{\beta,\gamma}E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\beta}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\gamma} \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}t_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{vis},\beta}(1+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}}{(1+r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{vis},\beta}r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\beta}(r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{vis},\beta}+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{vis},\beta})e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}t_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(1+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}}{(1+r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{ir},\gamma}r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\gamma}(r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{ir},\gamma}+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{ir},\gamma})e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}} \\
&\quad\times \frac{t_{m1,\mathrm{SF},s}e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}(1+r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{SF},s}r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{SF},s})}{1+r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{SF},s}r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{SF},s}+r_{1m,\mathrm{SF},s}(r_{mm^{\prime\prime},\mathrm{SF},s}+r_{m^{\prime\prime}2,\mathrm{SF},s})e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}h_m}}L^{-}_{2/m,s,y}\chi_{y\beta\gamma}
\end{aligned}\tag{9b}
$$
