逆矩阵 $(E-A)^{-1}$ 如下。为避免原文横向拆开的三列被误读为两个矩阵，这里将九个元素分别列出；$M_{ij}$ 仅为译者的排版标识，不是新增物理量。

$$
(E-A)^{-1}=\frac{1}{\Delta}
\begin{pmatrix}M_{11}&M_{12}&M_{13}\\M_{21}&M_{22}&M_{23}\\M_{31}&M_{32}&M_{33}\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{2.7}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{11}={}1-\alpha_{m'}-\alpha_{m''}-\beta_{mm'}-\beta_{m'm''}-\gamma_{mm''}\\
+\alpha_m\alpha_{m'}+\alpha_{m'}\alpha_{m''}+\alpha_m\alpha_{m''}\\
+\alpha_m\beta_{m'm''}+\alpha_{m''}\beta_{mm'}-\alpha_m\alpha_{m'}\alpha_{m''},\\[4pt]
M_{12}={}(1-\alpha_{m''}-\beta_{m'm''}-\gamma_{mm''})r_{m1}r_{m'm''}t_{m'm}a_{m'}a_m^2\\
+(\alpha_{m'}+\beta_{mm'})r_{m1}r_{m''2}t_{m''m'}t_{m'm}t_{mm'}a_{m''}^2a_{m'}a_m^2,\\[4pt]
M_{13}={}r_{mm'}r_{m''2}t_{m''m'}t_{m'm}a_{m''}a_{m'}a_m^2,\\[4pt]
M_{21}={}(\alpha_m-\alpha_m\alpha_{m''})t_{mm'}a_{m'},\\[4pt]
M_{22}={}1-\alpha_{m''}-\beta_{m'm''}-\gamma_{mm''}
+\alpha_m\alpha_{m''}+\alpha_m\beta_{m'm''},\\[4pt]
M_{23}={}(1-\alpha_m)r_{m'm}r_{m''2}t_{m''m'}a_{m''}a_{m'}\\
+r_{m1}r_{m''2}t_{m''m'}t_{mm'}t_{m'm}a_{m''}a_{m'}^2a_m^2,\\[4pt]
M_{31}={}\alpha_m t_{mm'}t_{m'm''}a_{m''}a_{m'},\\[4pt]
M_{32}={}(\alpha_{m'}+\beta_{mm'}-\alpha_m\alpha_{m'})t_{m'm''}a_{m''},\\[4pt]
M_{33}={}1-\alpha_m-\alpha_{m'}-\beta_{mm'}-\alpha_m\alpha_{m'}.
\end{aligned}
$$

> **译者注（Q034）：** 式（2.7）按底本九个元素重排，未据逆矩阵性质修正原式。$M_{11}$、$M_{13}$、$M_{33}$ 等与式（2.3）的代数衔接尚待独立核验。旧译将第三列排成游离的三行，现恢复其所属行列；$M_{23}$ 首项的 $a_{m'}$ 次数亦已按底本恢复。

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta = 1 - \alpha_m - \alpha_{m'} - \alpha_{m''} - \beta_{mm'} - \beta_{m'm''} - \gamma_{mm''} \\
       \quad + \alpha_m \alpha_{m'} + \alpha_{m'} \alpha_{m''} + \alpha_m \alpha_{m''} + \alpha_m \beta_{m'm''} + \alpha_{m''} \beta_{mm'} \\
&- \alpha_m \alpha_{m'} \alpha_{m''} \\[4pt]
       = \begin{aligned}&(1-r_{m1}r_{mm'}a_m^2)(1-r_{m''m'}r_{m''2}a_{m''}^2)\\&-(r_{m'm}+r_{m1}a_m^2)(r_{m'm''}+r_{m''2}a_{m''}^2)a_{m'}^2\end{aligned}
\end{aligned} \tag{2.8}
$$

因此：

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(r_{mm'}a_m,\;r_{m'm''}t_{m'm}a_{m'}a_m,\;r_{m''2}t_{m''m'}t_{m'm}a_{m''}a_{m'}a_m)(E-A)^{-1}\\
&\qquad=(C_1,C_2,C_3).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.9}
$$

这里用译者排版标识 $C_1,C_2,C_3$ 分别列出原文行向量的三个分量，不改变分量次序或公共因子：

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_1={}&(1 - \alpha_{m'} - \alpha_{m''} - \beta_{mm'} - \beta_{m'm''} - \gamma_{mm''} + \alpha_{m'} \alpha_{m''} + \alpha_{m''} \beta_{mm'}) r_{mm'} a_m \\
&+ (\alpha_m - \alpha_m \alpha_{m''}) r_{m'm''} t_{m'm} t_{mm'} a_{m'}^2 a_m + \alpha_m r_{m''2} t_{m'm} t_{mm'} t_{m'm''} t_{m''m'} a_{m''}^2 a_{m'}^2 a_m, 
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_2={}&(1 - \alpha_{m''} - \beta_{m'm''} - \gamma_{mm''} - \alpha_m \gamma_{mm''}) r_{m'm''} t_{m'm} t_{mm'} a_{m'} a_m \\
&+ (\alpha_{m'} + \beta_{mm'} + \alpha_m \beta_{mm'}) r_{m''2} t_{m'm''} t_{m''m'} t_{m'm} a_{m''}^2 a_{m'} a_m, 
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_3={}&r_{m''2} t_{m''m'} t_{m'm} a_{m''} a_{m'} a_m
\end{aligned}
$$

> **译者注（Q034续）：** 底本式（2.9）右端未显式列出式（2.7）的公共因子 $1/\Delta$，这里照录；不能据此默认该因子已隐含或可省略。本式长行仅作分行排版，分量顺序保持为行向量。

确定所考虑的光的发光部位（$m$ 层、$m'$ 层或 $m''$ 层）及发光方向（下行光或上行光），即可求出对应的 $x_0$、$y_0$、$z_0$，进而使用 (2.6) 式和 (2.9) 式计算所需的电场振幅。
