## 4.2 和频极化

和频极化矢量 $\mathbf{P}$ 的坐标轴分量 $P_\alpha (\alpha = x, y, z)$ 由二阶非线性极化率张量 $\chi_{\alpha\beta\gamma}$ 以下式给出。其中下标 $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ 从左到右依次对应 SFG 光、可见光、红外光。

$$
P_\alpha = \sum_{\beta,\gamma} \chi_{\alpha\beta\gamma} E_{vis,\beta} E_{ir,\gamma}
\tag{4.25}
$$

下面按 SFG 光产生的不同部位分别考察来自薄膜的 和频极化。

### 4.2a. 1/m 界面外侧 ($x = -2n h_m\tan\theta_{m,SF}, z = 0^-$) 的 和频极化

突出在表面或稀疏分布的分子由表面电场极化。因此，由 (4.9) 式，点 $(x = -2n h_m\tan\theta_{m,SF}, z = 0^-)$ 处的极化由下式给出：

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\alpha(0^-, n) = &\sum_{\beta,\gamma} \chi_{\alpha\beta\gamma} E^0_{vis,\beta} E^0_{ir,\gamma}
\frac{(1+ r_{1m,vis,\beta})(1+ r_{m2,vis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m})(1+ r_{1m,ir,\gamma})(1+ r_{m2,ir,\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m})}
{(1 + r_{1m,vis,\beta} r_{m2,vis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m})(1+ r_{1m,ir,\gamma} r_{m2,ir,\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m})} \\
&\times \exp[i 2n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(-k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir} - k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis})]\big\} \\
= &P_\alpha(0^-, n=0) \exp[i 2n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(-k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir} - k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis})]\big\}
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.26a}
$$

然而，当表面形成完整薄膜时，分子所感受到的电场及极化需将薄膜记为 $m'$，按下式进行改写：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{vis,\alpha}(h_{m'}) E_{ir,\beta}(h_{m'}) = E^0_{vis,\alpha} E^0_{ir,\beta}
&\frac{t_{1m',vis,\alpha} (1+ r_{m'm,vis,\alpha})(1 + r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m})}
{1 + r_{1m',vis,\alpha} r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + (r_{m'm,vis,\alpha} + r_{1m',vis,\alpha}) r_{m2,vis,\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m}} \\
\times &\frac{t_{1m',ir,\beta} (1+ r_{m'm,ir,\beta})(1 + r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m})}
{1+ r_{1m',ir,\beta} r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + (r_{m'm,ir,\beta} + r_{1m',ir,\beta}) r_{m2,ir,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m}} \\
\times &\exp[-2i n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir} + k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis})]
\end{aligned}
$$

由此：

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\alpha(0^-) = &\sum_{\beta,\gamma} \chi_{\alpha\beta\gamma} E^0_{vis,\beta} E^0_{ir,\gamma}
\frac{t_{1m',vis,\beta} (1+ r_{m'm,vis,\beta})(1 + r_{m2,vis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m})}
{1+ r_{1m',vis,\beta} r_{m'm,vis,\beta} + (r_{m'm,vis,\beta} + r_{1m',vis,\beta}) r_{m2,vis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m}} \\
\times &\frac{t_{1m',ir,\gamma} (1+ r_{m'm,ir,\gamma})(1 + r_{m2,ir,\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m})}
{1+ r_{1m',ir,\gamma} r_{m'm,ir,\gamma} + (r_{m'm,ir,\gamma} + r_{1m',ir,\gamma}) r_{m2,ir,\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m}} \\
\times &\exp[-2i n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir} + k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis})] \\
= &P_\alpha(0^-, n=0) \exp[-2i n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir} + k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis})]
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.26b}
$$

### 4.2b. 1/m 界面内侧 ($x = -2n h\tan\theta_{m,SF}, z = 0^+$) 的 和频极化

薄膜内部，或气液界面液体侧的分子的极化，由 (4.13) 式以下式给出：

$$
\begin{aligned}

{(1 + r_{1m,vis,\beta} r_{m2,vis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m})(1+ r_{1m,ir,\gamma} r_{m2,ir,\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m})} \\
&\times \exp[i 2n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(-k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir} - k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis})] \\
= &P_\alpha(0^+, n=0) \exp[i 2n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(-k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir} - k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis})]
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.27}
$$

### 4.2d. m/2 界面内侧 ($x = -(2n + 1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,SF}, z = h_m^-$) 的 和频极化

P_\alpha(h_m^-) = &\sum_{\beta,\gamma} \chi_{\alpha\beta\gamma} E^0_{vis,\beta} E^0_{ir,\gamma}e

### 4.2e. 薄膜体相 [$0 < z < h_m$] 的 和频极化

对 $A_{n=0,1,2} : x = -2n h_m\tan\theta_{m,SF} - z\tan\theta_{m,SF}$ 及 $B_{n=-1,0,1,2} : x = -(2n + 1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,SF} + z\tan\theta_{m,SF}$，由 (4.21) 式以下式给出。

[$B_n$]: $E^+$ 源点

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\alpha(z) = &\sum_{\beta,\gamma} \chi_{\alpha\beta\gamma} E^0_{vis,\beta} E^0_{ir,\gamma}
\frac{t_{1m,vis,\beta} t_{1m,ir,\gamma}}
{(1+ r_{1m,vis,\beta} r_{m2,vis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m})(1 + r_{1m,ir,\gamma} r_{m2,ir,\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m})} \\
&\times \exp[-2i h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis} + k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir})] \\
&\times \{\exp\bigl[iz\bigl(\begin{aligned}&k_{m,vis}\cos\theta_{m,vis}(1+ \tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,vis})\\&+ k_{m,ir}\cos\theta_{m,ir}(1 + \tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,ir})\end{aligned}\bigr)\bigr] \\
&+ r_{m2,ir,\gamma} e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m} \exp\bigl[iz\bigl(\begin{aligned}&k_{m,vis}\cos\theta_{m,vis}(1 + \tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,vis})\\&- k_{m,ir}\cos\theta_{m,ir}(1-\tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,ir})\end{aligned}\bigr)\bigr] \\
&+ r_{m2,vis,\beta} e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m} \exp\bigl[iz\bigl(\begin{aligned}&-k_{m,vis}\cos\theta_{m,vis}(1-\tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,vis})\\&+ k_{m,ir}\cos\theta_{m,ir}(1 + \tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,ir})\end{aligned}\bigr)\bigr] \\
&+ r_{m2,vis,\beta} r_{m2,ir,\gamma} e^{2i(\beta_{m,vis}+\beta_{m,ir})h_m} \\
&\quad \times \exp\bigl[iz\bigl(\begin{aligned}&-k_{m,vis}\cos\theta_{m,vis}(1-\tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,vis})\\&- k_{m,ir}\cos\theta_{m,ir}(1-\tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,ir})\end{aligned}\bigr)\bigr]\} \\
&\times \exp[-i 2n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis} + k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir})] \\
= &P_\alpha(z, n=0) \exp[-i 2n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis} + k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir})]
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.30a}
$$

[$A_n$]: $E^-$ 源点

由 (4.24) 式以下式给出：

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\alpha(z) = &\sum_{\beta,\gamma} \chi_{\alpha\beta\gamma} E^0_{vis,\beta} E^0_{ir,\gamma}
\frac{t_{1m,vis,\beta} t_{1m,ir,\gamma}}
{(1+ r_{1m,vis,\beta} r_{m2,vis,\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m})(1 + r_{1m,ir,\gamma} r_{m2,ir,\gamma}e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m})} \\
&\times \{\exp\bigl[iz\bigl(\begin{aligned}&k_{m,vis}\cos\theta_{m,vis}(1-\tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,vis})\\&+ k_{m,ir}\cos\theta_{m,ir}(1-\tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,ir})\end{aligned}\bigr)\bigr] \\
&+ r_{m2,ir,\gamma} e^{2i\beta_{m,ir}h_m} \exp\bigl[iz\bigl(\begin{aligned}&k_{m,vis}\cos\theta_{m,vis}(1-\tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,vis})\\&- k_{m,ir}\cos\theta_{m,ir}(1+ \tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,ir})\end{aligned}\bigr)\bigr] \\
&+ r_{m2,vis,\beta} e^{2i\beta_{m,vis}h_m} \exp\bigl[iz\bigl(\begin{aligned}&-k_{m,vis}\cos\theta_{m,vis}(1 + \tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,vis})\\&+ k_{m,ir}\cos\theta_{m,ir}(1-\tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,ir})\end{aligned}\bigr)\bigr] \\
&+ r_{m2,vis,\beta} r_{m2,ir,\gamma} e^{2i(\beta_{m,vis}+\beta_{m,ir})h_m} \\
&\quad \times \exp\bigl[iz\bigl(\begin{aligned}&-k_{m,vis}\cos\theta_{m,vis}(1+ \tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,vis})\\&- k_{m,ir}\cos\theta_{m,ir}(1 + \tan\theta_{m,SF}\tan\theta_{m,ir})\end{aligned}\bigr)\bigr]\} \\
&\times \exp[-i 2n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis} + k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir})] \\
= &P_\alpha(z, n=0) \exp[-i 2n h_m \tan\theta_{m,SF}(k_{m,vis}\sin\theta_{m,vis} + k_{m,ir}\sin\theta_{m,ir})]
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.30b}
$$
