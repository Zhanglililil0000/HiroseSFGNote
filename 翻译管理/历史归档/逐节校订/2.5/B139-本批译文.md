## 5.1 L 系数、电场振幅

L 系数是联系极化与由其产生的光的电场振幅的系数。

$$
E_{SF,\alpha} = \sum_{\beta} L_{\alpha\beta} P_\beta^{SF}
$$

根据推广的 斯涅尔 折射定律，产生的 SFG 光同时包含上行（−）光和上行（+）光（原文称谓）。此外，L 系数的表达式因极化位置不同而异。原始推导的公式考虑了无限薄薄膜 m 极化后出射到介质 1 和介质 2 中的光，将 s 偏振和 p 偏振的电场振幅与极化的 x、y、z 分量联系在一起。此处对此进行扩展。另外，共同因子 $4\pi i \omega_{SF}/c$（当使用波数矢量代替折射率时为 $4\pi i \omega_{SF}^2/c^2$）在此省略。

L 系数的记法：$L_{i/j, s(p), \alpha}$ 表示当极化片 $m'$ 夹在 $i$ 层与 $j$ 层之间时，极化的 $\alpha$ 分量（$\alpha = x, y, z$）到其产生的光中 s 偏振或 p 偏振分量（非坐标轴分量）的系数。上行（−）光与上行（+）光（原文称谓）的区分，以上标 $-$ 和 $+$ 表示。

对于位于介质 1 与层叠薄膜 m 之间的极化片 $m'$ 所产生的光：

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{1/m,p,x} &= \cos\theta_{m,SF}/(n_{1,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{1,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.1a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{1/m,s,y} &= 1/(n_{1,SF}\cos\theta_{1,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.1b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{1/m,p,z} &= (n_{m,SF}/n_{m',SF})\sin\theta_{m',SF}/(n_{1,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{1,SF}) \\
               &= (n_{m,SF}/n_{m',SF})^2 \sin\theta_{m,SF}/(n_{1,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{1,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.1c}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^+_{1/m,p,x} &= \cos\theta_{1,SF}/(n_{1,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{1,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.2a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^+_{1/m,s,y} &= 1/(n_{1,SF}\cos\theta_{1,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.2b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^+_{1/m,p,z} &= -(n_{1,SF}/n_{m',SF})\sin\theta_{m',SF}/(n_{1,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{1,SF}) \\
               &= -(n_{1,SF}/n_{m',SF})^2 \sin\theta_{1,SF}/(n_{1,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{1,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.2c}
$$

对于位于介质 2 与层叠薄膜 m 之间的极化片 $m''$ 所产生的光：

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{2/m,p,x} &= \cos\theta_{2,SF}/(n_{2,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{2,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.3a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{2/m,s,y} &= 1/(n_{2,SF}\cos\theta_{2,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.3b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{2/m,p,z} &= (n_{2,SF}/n_{m'',SF})\sin\theta_{m'',SF}/(n_{2,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{2,SF}) \\
               &= (n_{2,SF}/n_{m'',SF})^2 \sin\theta_{2,SF}/(n_{2,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{2,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.3c}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^+_{2/m,p,x} &= \cos\theta_{m,SF}/(n_{2,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{2,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.4a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^+_{2/m,s,y} &= 1/(n_{2,SF}\cos\theta_{2,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.4b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^+_{2/m,p,z} &= -(n_{m,SF}/n_{m'',SF})\sin\theta_{m'',SF}/(n_{2,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{2,SF}) \\
               &= -(n_{m,SF}/n_{m'',SF})^2 \sin\theta_{m,SF}/(n_{2,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF} + n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{2,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.4c}
$$

对于层叠薄膜内部的极化片所产生的光：

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{m/m,p,x} &= L^+_{m/m,p,x} = \cos\theta_{m,SF}/(2 n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.5a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{m/m,s,y} &= L^+_{m/m,s,y} = 1/(2 n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.5b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
L^-_{m/m,p,z} &= -L^+_{m/m,p,z} = \sin\theta_{m,SF}/(2 n_{m,SF}\cos\theta_{m,SF})
\end{aligned}\tag{5.5c}
$$

由此，各部位产生的 SFG 光的电场振幅表示如下。

**来源于 $P(0)$ 的项**（$P(0^-)$ 为介质 1 侧电场引起的极化，$P(0^+)$ 为薄膜侧电场引起的极化）：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1,p}(z = 0) &= L^-_{1/m,p,x} P_x(0) + L^-_{1/m,p,z} P_z(0)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.6a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{1,s}(z = 0) &= L^-_{1/m,s,y} P_y(0)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.6b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{m,p}(z = 0) &= L^+_{1/m,p,x} P_x(0) + L^+_{1/m,p,z} P_z(0)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.7a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{m,s}(z = 0) &= L^+_{1/m,s,y} P_y(0)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.7b}
$$

**来源于 $P(h_m)$ 的项**（$P(h_m^+)$ 为介质 2 侧电场引起的极化，$P(h_m^-)$ 为薄膜侧电场引起的极化）：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{m,p}(z = h_m) &= L^-_{2/m,p,x} P_x(h_m) + L^-_{2/m,p,z} P_z(h_m)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.8a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{m,s}(z = h_m) &= L^-_{2/m,s,y} P_y(h_m)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.8b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{2,p}(z = h_m) &= L^+_{2/m,p,x} P_x(h_m) + L^+_{2/m,p,z} P_z(h_m)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.9a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{2,s}(z = h_m) &= L^+_{2/m,s,y} P_y(h_m)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.9b}
$$

**来源于 $P(z)$ 的项**：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{m,p}(z) &= L^-_{m/m,p,x} P_x(z) + L^-_{m/m,p,z} P_z(z)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.10a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^-_{m,s}(z) &= L^-_{m/m,s,y} P_y(z)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.10b}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{m,p}(z) &= L^+_{m/m,p,x} P_x(z) + L^+_{m/m,p,z} P_z(z)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.11a}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E^+_{m,s}(z) &= L^+_{m/m,s,y} P_y(z)
\end{aligned}\tag{5.11b}
$$
