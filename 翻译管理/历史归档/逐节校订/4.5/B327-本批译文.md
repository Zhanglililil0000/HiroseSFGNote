### 5.2 $\phi_A=\phi_B=0,\pi$（两个三角形均朝下或均朝上）

<!-- source p03l32 -->

当$\phi_A=\phi_B=0$时，$\cos\phi_A=\cos\phi_B=\cos2\phi_A=\cos2\phi_B=\cos3\phi_A=\cos3\phi_B=+1$；当$\phi_A=\phi_B=\pi$时，$\cos\phi_A=\cos\phi_B=\cos3\phi_A=\cos3\phi_B=-1$，$\cos2\phi_A=\cos2\phi_B=+1$。

<!-- source p04l01 -->

#### 全对称谱带

当$\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}\gg\beta_{\xi\xi\zeta},\beta_{\eta\eta\zeta}$时，$\beta_{aac}\sim(4/9)\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}$、$\beta_{ccc}\sim(1/9)\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}$。

**$a_{1}$**

<!-- source p04l02 -->

$$
\beta _{xxz} = -2(\beta _{aac} - \beta _{ccc})(\cos \alpha - \cos ^{3}\alpha ) + 2\beta _{aac}\cos \alpha
$$

<!-- source p04l03 -->

$$
\beta _{yyz} = +2\beta _{aac}\cos \alpha
$$

<!-- source p04l04 -->

$$
\beta _{zzz} = +2(\beta _{aac} - \beta _{ccc})(\cos \alpha - \cos ^{3}\alpha ) + 2\beta _{ccc}\cos \alpha
$$

**$b_{1}$**

<!-- source p04l05 -->

$$
\beta _{zxx} = -2(\beta _{aac} - \beta _{ccc})(\cos \alpha - \cos ^{3}\alpha )
$$

<!-- source p04l06 -->

$$
\beta _{xzx} = -2(\beta _{aac} - \beta _{ccc})(\cos \alpha - \cos ^{3}\alpha )
$$

#### 简并谱带

当$\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}\gg\beta_{\xi\xi\zeta},\beta_{\eta\eta\zeta}$时，$\beta_{caa}\sim(4/9)\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}$、$\beta_{aaa}\sim(4\sqrt2/9)\beta_{\zeta\zeta\zeta}$。

<!-- source p04l08 -->

（$\beta_{zyy}$和$\beta_{yzy}$由$b$轴方向振动贡献，其他分量由$a$轴方向振动贡献。）

<!-- source p04l09 -->

（$\pm$的上号对应$\phi_A=\phi_B=0$，下号对应$\phi_A=\phi_B=\pi$。）

**$a_{1}$**

<!-- source p04l10 -->

$$
\beta _{xxz} = 2[-2\beta _{caa}(\cos \alpha - \cos ^{3}\alpha ) \pm \beta _{aaa}(\sin \alpha - \sin ^{3}\alpha )]
$$

<!-- source p04l11 -->

$$
\beta _{yyz} = -(\pm 2) \beta _{aaa}\sin \alpha
$$

<!-- source p04l12 -->

$$
\beta _{zzz} = 2[2\beta _{caa}(\cos \alpha - \cos ^{3}\alpha ) \pm \beta _{aaa}\sin ^{3}\alpha ]
$$

$$

<!-- source p04l14 -->

$$
\beta _{xzx} = -2[\beta _{caa}(\cos \alpha - \cos ^{3}\alpha ) \pm \beta _{aaa}(\sin \alpha - \sin ^{3}\alpha )]
$$
