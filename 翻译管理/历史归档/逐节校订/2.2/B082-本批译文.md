#### 4.1d. $1/m/2$ 体系中 $1/m$ 界面薄膜侧的激发电场

位置为 $x=-2nh_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}$、$z=0^+$。这里讨论岛状覆盖层或单分子层（1 ML，折射率为 $n$）表面正下方的分子所感受到的电场。将附录C给出的膜内上行（−）光与下行（+）光电场相加，即得所需电场。

对于附着在足够厚的基板上的薄膜层，有：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}(0^+)
&=E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}
\left(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}\right)}
{1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[-ik_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\,2nh_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.10}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}(0^+)
&=E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\left(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}\right)}
{1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[-ik_{m,\mathrm{ir}}\,2nh_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.11}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}(0^+)E_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}(0^+)\\
&=E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\left(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}\right)
\left(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}\right)}
{\left(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}\right)
\left(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}\right)}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[i\,2nh_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}
\left(-k_{m,\mathrm{ir}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}-k_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\right)\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.12}
$$

#### 4.1e. $1/m/2$ 体系中 $m/2$ 界面介质2侧的激发电场

位置为 $x=-(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}$、$z=h_m^+$。产生极化的是透射而来的下行（+）光电场。

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}(h_m^+)
&=E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}t_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}}
{1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[-ik_{m,\mathrm{vis}}(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.13}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}(h_m^+)
&=E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}t_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}}
{1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[-ik_{m,\mathrm{ir}}(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.14}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}(h_m^+)E_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}(h_m^+)\\
&=E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}t_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}
t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}t_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}
e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}
{\left(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}\right)
\left(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}\right)}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[i(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}
\left(-k_{m,\mathrm{ir}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}-k_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\right)\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.15}
$$

#### 4.1f. $1/m/2$ 体系中 $m/2$ 界面薄膜侧的激发电场

位置为 $x=-(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}$、$z=h_m^-$。电场为上行（−）光与下行（+）光电场之和。

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}(h_m^-)
&=E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}\left(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}\right)e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}}
{1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[-ik_{m,\mathrm{vis}}(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.16}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}(h_m^-)
&=E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}\left(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}\right)e^{\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}}
{1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[-ik_{m,\mathrm{ir}}(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.17}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}(h_m^-)E_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}(h_m^-)\\
&=E^0_{\mathrm{vis},\alpha}E^0_{\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\frac{t_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}t_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}
\left(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}\right)\left(1+r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}\right)
e^{i(\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}+\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}})h_m}}
{\left(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}r_{m2,\mathrm{vis},\alpha}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}h_m}\right)
\left(1+r_{1m,\mathrm{ir},\beta}r_{m2,\mathrm{ir},\beta}e^{2i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}\right)}\\
&\quad\times\exp\!\left[i(2n+1)h_m\tan\theta_{m,\mathrm{SF}}
\left(-k_{m,\mathrm{ir}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}-k_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\sin\theta_{m,\mathrm{vis}}\right)\right].
\end{aligned}\tag{4.18}
$$

> **译校注（Q059，保留原式）：** 式（4.17）分子在底本p7写作 $e^{\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}$，未见虚数单位 $i$；而式（4.16）和乘积式（4.18）保留相位因子中的 $i$。这里按原式转写，候选 $e^{i\beta_{m,\mathrm{ir}}h_m}$ 仅列于本注，未经确认不替换。原式图保留在本批对照记录中。
